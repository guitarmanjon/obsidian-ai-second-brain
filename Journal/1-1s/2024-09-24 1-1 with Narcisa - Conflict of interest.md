## Attendees

## Purpose

- [[Narcisa|NP]] declared a potential conflict of interest to JS, her line manager, and JS followed up to understand the situation fully.

## Key Discussion Points

- On 23/09/2024, [[Narcisa|NP]] declared to JS (her line manager) that she was in a personal (romantic) relationship with another individual within IMG, as per HR guidelines. On the face of it, JS saw no conflict of interest (or potential for one), so didn't feel the need to escalate to HR at that point.
- On 24/09/2024, JS spoke to the other individual's line manager to clarify his position. During that conversation JS learned the relationship had been going on since 2023, and that [[Narcisa|NP]] had been involved in the selection process for a position [[YB]] applied for and was a candidate for - this then becomes a conflict of interest.
- JS spoke with [[Narcisa|NP]] on 24/09/2024 (around 14:30) to better understand the situation. Per her explanation:
	- She believed she was being asked to take part in a screening process, rather than an actual interview, in which [[YB]] was a candidate.
	- She didn't feel comfortable telling the organiser about her personal relationship, wanting to keep it private.
	- During the screening process she did not score any candidates or influence the organiser on the outcome - she asked her assigned questions and took notes on responses consistently across all candidates. Scoring was carried out by other panel members without her input.
	- She is aware this constitutes a potential conflict of interest but does not believe the outcome of the selection process was influenced towards any particular candidate because of the relationship.
- From JS's perspective: satisfied there was no influence on the outcome, and that [[Narcisa|NP]] is aware of the potential conflict of interest here.

## Decisions

- JS accepted [[Narcisa|NP]]'s account and was satisfied there had been no actual influence on the selection outcome, while noting the conflict of interest for the record.
