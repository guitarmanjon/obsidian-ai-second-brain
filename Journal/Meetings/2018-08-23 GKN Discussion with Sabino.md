## Key Discussion Points

- Need to be careful around IP - be clear on what already exists in AS projects so GKN cannot claim IP.
- Key indicator of distortion is post-machining measurement.
	- Could look at contactless options, e.g. GOM, or SPRINT scanning.
	- Distortion can occur when parts are unclamped.
	- Geometric aspects of the part are key for modelling; residual stresses also key.
- Could strain gauges be placed at fixture contact points, e.g. to indicate how much a part is pushing/pulling against a fixture, as an indicator of how much it will distort after fixture release.
- Question over correlation of on-machine inspection data with CMM data.
- Inspection techniques: [[SA]] believes some companies lack the knowledge of how to properly inspect components, which can lead to conflict between manufacturers and OEMs (e.g. GKN inspect and consider a part OK, but Airbus inspect differently and potentially reject it). Suggestion of a supply chain data link to OEMs to share inspection methodology.
