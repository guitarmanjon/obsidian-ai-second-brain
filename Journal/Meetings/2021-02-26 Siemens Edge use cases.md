## Attendees

- [[Siemens]]: [[Gerd Heinrich]], [[Nathaniel Hudson|Nat Hudson]], [[Rob Poyner]]
- AMRC: [[Jon S]]

## Purpose

- To make contact with [[Siemens]] for support on Edge use cases, review progress since earlier workshops, and agree next steps.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Gerd Heinrich]] (GH) - Aerospace Lead Consultant, part of Digital Industry area; works on digital enterprise/end-to-end solutions realising [[IIoT]] use cases ([[MES]], shop floor, etc.).
- [[Jon S]] gave an overview; [[Rob Poyner]] (RP) had sent use cases to GH ahead of the call.
- Objectives for the session: establish a support contact within [[Siemens]] (Gerd), and agree next steps following earlier workshops (including the [[PLM]] suite).
- Discussion of use cases from Rob's list, with emphasis on complementing existing activities rather than reinventing the wheel:
  - **Line 10 - Machine tool fingerprinting** (raised by [[Jon S]]): stood out to Gerd. [[Jon S]] explained the use case. GH noted [[Siemens]] has a team almost dedicated to this (a quality requirement) and offered to connect AMRC with [[Siemens]] experts. [[Siemens]] have built digital twins of machines with soft sensors, derived from machine builder data and direct machine-builder relationships; lots of existing collaborations in this field. Would use Edge data to derive a digital twin. GH could organise a specific call with Edge and machine-learning experts.
  - **Force feedback work** (Pete) - briefly discussed and explained.
  - **Line 8 - Evaluation of composite quality** (Oli Hayes): [[Siemens]] have done similar work with Airbourne in the Netherlands; a session could be arranged to discuss further.
  - **Line 12 - Digitisation of route card creation** (Oli Hayes): GH linked this to [[Siemens]]' trusted traceability work, built on blockchain technology, applied in food and beverage for ingredients/processes; [[Siemens]] interested in taking it further.
  - **Line 14 - Process-to-process data flow for optimisation** (Kev Kerrigan): relates to composites, data flow through the production process; closely linked to Line 8.
- Discussion on how to take these forward: as part of larger streams of work (e.g. brought into SMR), as work packages in existing/future projects, and ultimately as demonstrations for [[Siemens]] to help get the message out.

## Decisions

- [[Gerd Heinrich]] to be the AMRC's support contact within [[Siemens]] going forward.
- [[Gerd Heinrich]] to organise a specific follow-up call on machine tool fingerprinting, bringing in [[Siemens]]' Edge and machine-learning experts.

## Action Items

- [[Gerd Heinrich]] - (Organise a call with [[Siemens Edge]] and [[Machine Learning|ML]] experts to discuss machine tool fingerprinting.)
- [[Gerd Heinrich]] / [[Oli Hayes]] - (Arrange a session to discuss composite quality evaluation, referencing [[Siemens]]' prior work with Airbourne in the Netherlands.)
