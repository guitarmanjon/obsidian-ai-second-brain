## Attendees

- [[Jon Stammers|JS]] (working notes, following the preferred candidate for the Machining KTP Associate declining the offer)

## Purpose

- Working notes assessing the impact of not having a Machining KTP Associate (Associate 1), after the preferred candidate withdrew, and building the case for continuing with only the Digital Associate ([[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]]).

## Key Discussion Points

- From the Associate 2 (digital) original workplan, items relying on Associate 1 (machining):
	- 1.4 - Initial discussion/requirements capture with Associate 1 and KB supervisor around machining data/connectivity needs. Impact: none - already beyond this point, conversations with appropriate Boeing staff can fulfil it.
	- 5.3 - Determine data capture/analysis requirements for long-term implementation of subtractive methods with Associate 1. Impact: this work doesn't exist without Associate 1.
	- 5.4 - Collaborate with Associate 1 to prepare a data capture plan for subtractive machining optimisation. Impact: would have primarily supported Associate 1's own dev work - no impact on Associate 2, who has enough work.
	- 5.5 - Develop data/analytics pipelines for control measures developed by Associate 1. Impact: no Associate 1, no work needed here - other pipelines will exist beyond this one.
	- 5.8 - Support Associate 1 in training activities around data use best practice for machining optimisation. Impact: no Associate 1, no training to deliver on this specifically, but Associate 2 will have other training to deliver.
	- Stage 5 is the main crossover with Associate 1, amounting to ~3 months of effort. Some of this work can continue anyway with input from relevant Boeing colleagues and capturing data from Boeing machine tools.
- 29/07/2025 brief chat with [[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]]: getting data and support from MEs in Boeing Sheffield from machine tools; will support the stability of Boeing Sheffield even though machining capability development isn't on the cards; already having an impact into BS without the Machining KTP.
- "Blurb for Andy" (letter drafted to [[Andy Barnett|Andy]] explaining the decision): the preferred candidate for the Machining KTP Associate informed AMRC he would no longer be accepting the offer, pursuing other avenues. This prompted frank, open internal discussions and with Boeing UK, leading to a decision to continue the KTP with only one Associate ([[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]]). Reasons given:
	- Re-hiring now would be challenging, even with an IUK deadline extension.
	- Boeing Sheffield's situation has changed significantly since the original application - back then process optimisation was very desirable (which the Machining KTP would have addressed), but the business has since shifted focus to process stability, which doesn't offer the opportunities/support needed for an adventurous KTP developing new machining processes; it would also be a struggle to get the business to support such activities in the current climate.
	- Digital solutions and analytics are being embraced at Boeing Sheffield, and [[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]] is being welcomed into wider team discussions - machining activities aren't the only target for her skills/work.
	- Transition routes for machining-related improvements are now jeopardised by increased regulation and scrutiny.
	- Machining optimisation activities can't take place at Boeing Sheffield; AMRC funding for such activities is limited; other tandem project opportunities were explored but not successful, leaving very limited scope for machining activities within the KTP.
	- Considered financial impact: the original £20m scope is reduced due to decreased scale of BUKS operations post-pandemic/downturn; the original application estimated time savings from both process optimisation and machining cycle optimisation - machining cycle time improvements will be difficult given the stability focus and regulatory pressures, but digital time savings/optimisation will remain due to broader impact on overall process/operational efficiency.
	- Interdependence between the KTPs: originally, stage 5 of the Digital KTP was the main interaction point with the Machining KTP (~3 months effort for that stage, ~1.5 months collaborative effort across the 3-year KTP). Most stage 5 activities will happen anyway (and in many cases already are), even without the Machining KTP, because the Manufacturing Engineering team at Boeing Sheffield are already very engaged with [[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]]'s work, including providing machining data sets. The Industrial Engineering team are also engaged with [[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]], looking at the wider systems picture. Existing digital machining activities at AMRC (a collaboration with Boeing and others) share outputs with the KTP team. The "One AMRC" approach means machining input is successfully channelled through IMG into Boeing Sheffield.
	- Conclusion: continuing with only the Digital work with [[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]] should lead to a successful KTP outcome for Boeing UK and AMRC (and [[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]]); the impact of not pursuing the Machining KTP is minimal, and if anything reduces overall project risk and removes the potential for the project running 4+ years in total.

## Decisions

- Decision to continue the KTP with only the Digital Associate ([[Chaitanya Krishnakumar|Chaitanya]]), rather than re-recruiting for the Machining Associate role.
