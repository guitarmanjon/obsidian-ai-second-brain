## Key Discussion Points

- No structural parts (aluminium in particular) can currently be recycled because of certification constraints.
- Seat parts can be recycled - this is the target product identified by [[AB]] (Andy B).
- [[Chris Taylor|CT]] not sure about a physical demo due to a £100k cap on TMG contribution; a CAD/CAM demonstrator might be more feasible and a good demo.
- Lime Centre (Brunel) is looking at recycling of metallic materials in aerospace.
- [[AB]]: additive manufacturing is very un-environmentally friendly and energy expensive.
- Tool materials and fluids are of interest to [[Chris Taylor|CT]].
- [[AB]]: consider the full manufacturing cycle.
- [[AB]]: cobalt is a big risk - used as a binder for most tools; productivity would fall back to 1950s levels without cobalt-bound tools, and there is no replacement material for cobalt.
- A study is looking at potential eco-machining solutions vs. the reality, e.g. oil-based lubricants vs. CO2 (lots of carbon release with CO2; cutting fluids are used within a machine for around 6 months).
- MQL (minimum quantity lubrication) discussed:
  - Advanced MQL system with comprehensive monitoring and feedback?
  - MSL type 2 system.
  - Environmental impact process optimisation?
