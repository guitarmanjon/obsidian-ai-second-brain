## Purpose

- Catch-up covering general wellbeing of CWF, digital work, Copilot use amongst staff, and future plans in this domain.

## Key Discussion Points

- How are things: busy! Still struggling with planning. Previous planner, [[Daniil Smeliov|Dan]], took an approach of planning to the last minute, very detailed - Dan left a couple of years ago and CWF has struggled to find a replacement. EFACS (planning software) isn't helping, as it's out of date. Complexity of operations makes it challenging, but the background mathematics hasn't changed, e.g. MRP. Data input is usually the source of errors, or the planner not fully understanding the challenge. Ops Director hired 4 months ago has had a positive impact, bringing the relevant teams together. A massive spreadsheet has been running operations for many years. Planning issues have led to arrears with customers.
- One-day workshop with [[Katie]] to reshape CWF data strategy - great workshop, lots of prep put into it. Report from Katie with some next steps. Looked at a holistic view across the company, various databases, disparate systems - initially based on her KTP report, updated with the current setup and pain points from current leadership. Identified that most of the data needed to tackle the planning challenge is already there.
- [[AI]] tools - [[Katie]] talked about HSBC developing their own models for internal uses. Being used internally at CWF for reporting and code writing.
- [[Rufino Bolado]] attended the [[HVMC]] roadmap launch at AMRC - [[Neil Tatman]] left an impression with some scathing comments about the roadmaps and their implementation. [[Rab Scott]] asked about CWF using [[AI]] for planning/production scheduling, rather than just Copilot tools. Rufino mentioned archsys.io (a link from a secondary-school friend) - looks like a great solution but still requires a foundational data layer. Rufino described what he'd expect from a software solutions provider, like [[Razor]], is that they fill you with confidence they'll deliver a robust solution and have done it before - not his experience with [[Razor]]. How can the likes of [[Rolls-Royce]] influence the take-up and development of technology?
- Data stewardship - Rufino thinks that some elements of manufacturing data should be easy to simulate.
