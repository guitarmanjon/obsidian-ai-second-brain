## Purpose

- Follow-up on the AMRC AI strategy work started at the May 2024 workshop: review progress, revisit the AI vision statement, and plan next steps (project repository, CMI links, Data and AI Showcase, AMRC AI survey).

## Key Discussion Points

- Alignment to the government's AI Opportunities Action Plan flagged by [[Jonathan Eyre]] as now needed, since that's where funding will be focused ([[Rab Scott]] agreed); [[Rab Scott]] queried whether a 5-year horizon is realistic.
- [[Jonathan Eyre]]: what matters is understanding how to deploy - working with vendors to get real benefits in industry - not necessarily doing the deployment itself, otherwise questioned what the impact/point of looking at AI as a Catapult would even be.
- New CMI working group on digital twins forming, chaired by David Wagg, with [[Jonathan Eyre]] and Mila Mihaylova as vice-chairs.
- What are we up to across the AMRC?
  - Generally agreed it would be good to share more of what's happening across groups in the vast realm of AI - both current work and what's planned.
  - Use of a Google Form was proposed to capture basic project info; some pushback that Google Forms are too basic, though it would be quick to get something started.
  - More helpful would be to agree what info on project work should be captured (and consistently labelled) to enable future knowledge sharing/searching.
  - [[Lindsay Lee]] queried the phrase "development of deployable solutions"; [[Rab Scott]] followed up asking whether AMRC also helps SMEs adopt existing solutions (i.e. deployment help), and separately asked whether there's an index of AI-related PhDs/EngDs being supervised.
  - [[Lindsay Lee]] raised whether, under the current wording, AMRC is taking on responsibility for solutions remaining trustworthy and ethical; [[Jonathan Eyre]] clarified the wording is about understanding fundamentals, not about ensuring compliance or ongoing responsibility (unless AMRC chooses to spin that out as a service). He added that, per [[Lindsay Lee]]'s point, it may help to set out the categories of AI activity cared about before going out for a blanket understanding of where AI is being used.
- Review of the Vision developed at the May 2024 workshop:
  - Vision as defined: "The AMRC will become a global centre-of-excellence in the development and deployment of trustworthy and ethical AI solutions that realise value for the manufacturing sector by 2030."
  - Broadly OK, but detail is needed on how this will actually be achieved - agreed as the next challenge.
  - Lots of discussion on the semantics of the vision, particularly the word "deployment" - AMRC's work is more development and demonstration than deployment, which carries risks around ongoing maintenance and responsibility, though there are some examples of deployment.
  - Options considered: include all "3 Ds" (development, demonstration, deployment), or reword as "development of deployable solutions".
- CMI: trying to engage further and build a closer working relationship; James Marshall (CMI Director) due to visit AMRC in the coming months to look at better alignment.
- Data and AI Showcase: pencilled in for 13th May; all AMRC partners invited; purpose is to show partners the breadth and depth of AMRC's Data and AI work to stimulate further conversation and projects; call for papers imminent (though happy to take submissions already); anyone wanting to help organise is welcome to.
- AMRC Survey: plan to pull together a short survey to gauge who is working on "AI" and use of AI tools (and appetite for them) across the AMRC, partly to stimulate discussion towards adopting AI tools that could make everyday work easier (e.g. meeting notetakers).
- AOB: agreed these strategy catch-ups need to happen regularly.

## Decisions

- These AI strategy meetings should happen on a regular, recurring basis.
