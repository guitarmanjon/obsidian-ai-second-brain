## Purpose

- xAMRC [[AI]] Strategy Workshop, bringing together [[AI]]-related activity leads from across the AMRC to share what each group is doing and start shaping a collective [[AI]] identity/vision.

## Key Discussion Points

- Planned activities for the workshop:
  - Activity 1 - sharing of each group's projects and capabilities (25-30 mins), potentially "speed-dating" style: one person per group stays put while others rotate round for 3 minutes each, followed by 5 minutes of reflection. Overview slides to be printed to avoid relying on screens.
  - Activity 2 - moving group work (at least 50 mins): round tables with 4 groups discussing key questions (10 mins each before moving on, final group rationalises - 5 moves total). Questions: what are the key challenges manufacturing SMEs face implementing [[AI]] solutions? What are the key challenges manufacturing OEMs face implementing [[AI]] solutions? What can [[AI]] solve for manufacturers? Where can AMRC help, and where can't it? What do we want to help with, and what should we help with?
  - Activity 3 - future working groups / [[AI]] events: discuss the "7 strands of [[AI]]", whether a working group / Special Interest Group / Community of Practice would be valuable for each strand, and whether AMRC should aim for a Shef.[[AI]]-style community with meetups every 6 months.
  - "10 from 10" brainstorming activity (from workshopper.com) considered as a format: silent brainstorming, pick top ten, then vote - but needs a clear central question, e.g. "how do we improve AMRC's branding in the [[AI]] space?"
- Braindump ahead of the workshop:
  - Key challenge framing: work out AMRC's [[AI]] identity - what it does/doesn't want to be known for, and a vision for [[AI]] at AMRC; then decide what work it does/doesn't want to do. Is [[AI]] always the answer?
  - Proposed warm-up: active speed networking - walk around, and when told to stop, share something about yourself with the nearest person.
  - Homework for attendees: review [[AI]] areas, describe what your group does, note similarities to other groups.
  - Equipment needed: sticky notes, pens, voting dots, flip charts, sticky tape.
  - Resource referenced: workshopper.com.
