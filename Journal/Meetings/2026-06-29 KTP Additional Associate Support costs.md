## Attendees

- n/a - documents an email exchange with [[Louise Sharpe]], prompted by a question from [[Denzil]] in LMC 4.

## Purpose

- Clarify what the "Additional Associate Support" line item (~£190k) in the [[Boeing]] KTP finance statement covers, and whether it can fund access to [[University of Sheffield]] equipment/facilities.

## Key Discussion Points

- In LMC 4 (5 June 2025), [[Denzil]] asked what the ~£190k "Additional Associate Support" line covers, and whether it means access to [[University of Sheffield|UoS]] equipment paid for out of this budget.
- JS emailed [[Louise Sharpe]] on 16 June 2025: [[Boeing]] had raised this in the LMC and separately - the KTP candidate [[Boeing]] are most interested in (background in materials testing) knows the labs at the main university where this testing could be done, and [[Boeing]] want to know if this line item covers access to such facilities.
- [[Louise Sharpe]] didn't reply directly, but in a separate thread ("KTP recruitment update - subtractive processes", 20 June 2025) explained that at the application stage, "Associate estates costs" could have been requested: covers use of existing facilities and anything chargeable within the knowledge base (normally zero, as the associate is based at the business partner's premises); under FEC, the knowledge base partner can request up to £10,000 extra per project where the associate needs significant time using the knowledge base's facilities (labs, office space, specialist/chargeable equipment e.g. wind tunnels, electron microscopy) - the associate is expected to spend most of their time at the business, so extended time at the knowledge base is discouraged; justification required, approved/rejected by KTP programme management, no exceptions to the maximum; the business partner is encouraged to help cover costs over the standard amount. Using internal facilities outside of this would incur an internal cost - there's an overhead budget if AMRC agreed to it internally, but given the university's financial position this isn't currently advised; ultimately it comes down to the relationship with [[Boeing]] and whether it helps the project.
- JS pressed again on what "Additional Associate Support" (~£190k) itself covers; [[Louise Sharpe]] replied on 24 June 2025 that it's the standard FEC overhead costs covering the costs of managing associates - computing, personnel admin, learning resources, and secretariat support to the Local Management Committee. This is shown in the finance area of the application if [[Boeing]] want to see it.

## Decisions

- To access equipment, the correct route would have been to request funds under "Associate Estates costs" at the application stage (max £10,000 extra per project). "Additional Associate Support" itself is the [[University of Sheffield|UoS]] overhead that supports the KTP, not a facilities-access budget.
