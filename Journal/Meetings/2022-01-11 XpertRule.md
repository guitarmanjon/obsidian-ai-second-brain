## Purpose

- To discuss XpertRule's XpertFactory product, its overlap with Factory+, and potential collaboration on system integrators.

## Key Discussion Points

- XpertFactory has been rebranded for manufacturing and engineers to use directly; the main barrier to use is getting data into the right place and format.
- Discussion of talking to [[Alex]] about Factory+ - commercialising it to create a hardware platform, and overcoming the data challenge mentioned above.
- [[Iain Crosley]] (XR) is looking for system integrators to work with and deploy XpertFactory, with specialist integrators per sector - currently looking, possibly including Booth Welsh (pharma focused).
- Lack of integration identified as a barrier to adoption - both AMRC and XpertRule are looking for integrators, just approaching from different directions.
- Underneath, Viabl and XpertFactory are essentially the same product with different interfaces and target uses - Viabl is XpertRule configured in the cloud.
- A demo is available using Siemens LOGO, through the Shoestring project at IfM.
- [[Iain Crosley]]: modelling systems to generate preliminary data; can give AMRC the tools and training; has a good relationship with Siemens and uses Siemens-approved integrators, who often have the right skillset.
