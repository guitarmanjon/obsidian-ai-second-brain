## Purpose

- Introductory discussion with [[David Wagg]] to explore how AMRC and university faculty can complement each other on Digital Twin research and funding opportunities.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[David Wagg]] has a mechanical engineering background; on secondment to the Turing Institute as co-director of Digital Twin work alongside [[Keith Worden]].
- Has worked with [[Jon Eyre]] on Digital Twin extensively; there is a new CDT (Centre for Doctoral Training) on Digital Twins.
- Looking at where AMRC and the faculty can complement each other, not compete.
- UKRI Digital Twin transport sustainability call - Turing involved, with transport experts, UK-wide consortium; AMRC mentioned as part of the consortium.
- Three possible calls for a hub: a large UK-wide one, and a medium one (£9-12m) focused on AI with real-world data.
- Recent AI call for CDTs has a cap of 2 bids per institution. [[David Wagg]] is trying to work out whether his CDT proposal fits better under the AI call or the Engineering call - the university will decide when it needs to.
- Idea floated of a Sheffield-wide/regional AI hub, thematically based.
- Could SMEs be included? Yes, absolutely - the more the better.
- Letters of support envisaged from up to 50 companies, ranging from large companies to start-ups.
- Co-creation approach - input from companies on what the proposal will actually do.
- The initiative needs a leader or leadership team - open question of who has the time and energy to get involved.
- Potential narrative: AI for engineering, with low-TRL work from the University, translational work from AMRC, and implementation by companies.
- Regional hub, but needs UK-wide presence - NMIS, other HVMC centres, Huddersfield.
