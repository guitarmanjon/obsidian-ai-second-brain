## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Three potential architecture iterations considered: (a) Fourjaw Machinelink with Factory+; (b) bulk data output for hackathons; (c) real-time output of Fourjaw data.
- Option (a) is not progressing further at the moment, though could be investigated further.
- Option (b) is the current favourite.
- Option (c) is not feasible - Fourjaw unable to support it due to the work involved and other commitments.
- [[RD]] noted AMRC placed a large PO with Fourjaw (FJ) on the basis that an API was/would be available; [[RH]] not sure this was actually the case.
- Fourjaw uses a Mongo database. [[RD]] proposed looking at how to expose just a subset of Fourjaw data for the customers currently signed up.
- Streaming data is useful to the data champions as part of the learning process.
- A middle-ground streaming approach seems doable via an API.
