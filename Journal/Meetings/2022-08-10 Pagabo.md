- Discussion of use cases: knowing if someone has ditched their tag to go do something they shouldn't, or flagging if someone has been static too long (are they OK?).
- Discussed definition of research questions.
- Question raised on whether AMRC has access to aviation black-box accident data, or general studies on accident rates from such data - not confident AMRC has additional data here.
