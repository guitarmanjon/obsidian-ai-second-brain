## Purpose

- Discuss the IBM AC922 system, its capabilities, and how AMRC could make use of it with IBM's support.

## Key Discussion Points

- User experience is key - e.g. for installing and using software.
- AMRC has Linux experience internally, and can also get training via RSE if needed.
- IBM's Client Engineering team could assist, potentially via workshops with AMRC staff leading to an Apache "front end".
- Openshift raised as a possible option (https://www.ibm.com/cloud/openshift).
- PyTorch: CUDA available on Power 9, MMX (libraries) on Power 10; PyTorch models are interchangeable across these.
- Power 10 has very fast memory access.
- Discussion of integrating with IBM Cloud securely so that it still satisfies the needs of AMRC's partners - cores can be partitioned physically, according to US Military standards.
