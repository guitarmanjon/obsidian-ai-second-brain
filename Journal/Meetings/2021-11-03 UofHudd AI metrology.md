## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- AMPI is a big piece of work for UoH - UKRI-funded and funded under AMPI Strength in Places, with Rochdale funding for the building.
- Forward plan: machine builders develop new machines with AMPI, which then land at Catapult centres for exposure and testing.
- [[Ben M]] suggested more conversation is needed around AMPI and the Future Met Hub - mention the AFRC link in Mini-MISSY (WP12).
- AI/Metrology: Future Met Hub has a work package on this (WP8 - AI package); the researcher working on it is focused narrowly on surface metrology.
- Inputs during training, testing and use need to be traceable, including re-calibration; WP4 covers recalibration of sensors on machines.
- Discussed uncertainty measures, and the gap between what data scientists and engineers do - data scientists are efficient at finding an answer, while engineers can be inefficient because they want to understand details and build models.
- Also touched on inspection by exception, uncertainty in data and models, and traceability of evolutionary models.
- Raised the idea of joint feasibility hub funding, and a possible Powders hub in Sheffield.
- Mentioned [[Colme Higgins]] (Queen's University Belfast) working on sensorless condition monitoring using high-frequency data.
