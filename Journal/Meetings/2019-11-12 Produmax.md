## Purpose

- Meeting with [[Produmax]] to discuss their AS9145 zero-defect journey and scope how AMRC (Aerofoils, PM&C, [[Metrology]]) could collaborate with them and [[Boeing]] on data capture and inspection.

## Key Discussion Points

- AS9145 journey — working towards zero defect.
- Data capture; using the spindle probe for verification to minimise off-machine inspection.
- Mix of controllers in use — Fanuc, [[Siemens]], Heidenhain.
- Key data to capture: true position tolerance for gearbox casing and covers (15 μ), standard GD&T.
- Haven't yet considered differences in cycle time, spindle power, etc.
- Currently looking at temperature variation.
- A Cpk of 1.33 is needed to move towards zero defect.
- On-machine measurements differ from [[CMM]] measurements — want to be able to correlate [[CMM]] data to [[On-Machine Inspection|OMI]] data.
- [[Renishaw]] probes and software in use (I+ and Prod+).
- [[TMc]] proposed a 3-way AMRC approach: Aerofoils propose the inspection, PM&C capture the data, [[Metrology]] inform on what to do with the data.
- Potential 3-way collaboration between [[Produmax]], [[Boeing]], and AMRC.
