## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- SCHAD automation system already in place - used for knowledge management (videos, manuals, etc.) though not much used at present. Opening and closing jobs on the shop floor is the main current function.
- Predictive maintenance: predicting is fine but they want to know why errors are occurring. Spindle life is an issue.
- GE and Siemens have visited to look at connectivity - some success from Siemens, GE struggled with Mazak machines. DePuy want to know what they should be looking at.
- Struggling as they don't know what MTConnect tags are in the machine data - need to understand which are important.
- Want to be able to get OEE data and predictive maintenance/condition monitoring.
	- Suggested vibration monitoring supported by MT data, but they use high pressure coolant (70 bar) so have doubts over whether useful vibration signals could be detected over this.
	- Suggested fingerprint routine and fingerprint component - they have a component that accounts for ~20% of their machining time which could be a potential candidate. PCA could be used to identify outliers in the process signals.
- Discussion around monitoring kit and dashboard developed in Servitisation. [[Stephen Bowles]] mentioned the Legacy demo in F2050 and work done there.
