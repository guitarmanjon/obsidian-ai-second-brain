## Key Discussion Points

- [[Laurence Brooks]] is working on an EU proposal for "Explainable and Robust [[AI]]" under the [[AI]], Data and Robotics Partnership (Horizon CL4-2024-HUMAN-03, RIA), following on from meeting at the [[AI Ethics|Ethics]] of [[AI]] in Manufacturing event.
- Goal: enhance robustness, performance and reliability of [[AI]] systems, focused on explainability, transparency and accountability, via characterising both humans and [[AI]] and using that to build/manage dynamically interactive human-machine teams across contexts.
- Sheffield (Laurence) is coordinating the bid: ~€7m, 3-year duration, deadline 18 September 2024; 17 partners across 9 countries including Surrey, Southampton, and [[Siemens]] (Germany).
- One planned use case, "command and control", was to be led by [[BAE Systems]], but they've pulled out due to lack of capacity; Laurence asked whether anything in the AMRC portfolio could fit - so far only described as "Enhancing human-[[AI]] interaction in industrial/manufacturing tasks to improve decision-making and operational efficiency", leaving scope to shape it around an existing project or extension.
