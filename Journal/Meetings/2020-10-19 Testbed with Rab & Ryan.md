## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Overall: [[JS]] will be leading this project with a few different engineers working on it, including PM&C.
- Definitions (also available in the Innovation Hub doc from [[Rab]]):
  - Testbed — equipment and infrastructure to allow vendors to test their solutions; might have a generic dashboard to show the output of vendors' systems.
  - Demonstrator — just that: no interaction with potential vendors, shows a capability or art of the possible.
  - Sandpit — system to allow endpoint data capture and dashboard it; generates generic data for others to consume.
- Testbeds planned for:
  - I2.0 — legacy tools in F2050, improved dashboards and analytics.
  - I3.0 — "Full Monty," align with ABG, integrate into Factory+, generate datasets for perfect parts.
  - I4.0 — Sinumerik Edge on Starrag NB & ARMS (Titan).
  - A holistic testbed using the various cells in F2050 and the BMS.
- Other points:
  - Costings are set; actual details of scope can be adjusted.
  - One company engagement per line item on the finance sheet.
  - The testbeds are for Innovate UK to learn what works and what doesn't — supporting future calls for testbeds in 2021, including a Smart Factories call.
  - Possible additional funds for handholding companies that come in to AMRC.
- Budget:
  - £290k to be spent before end of March.
  - Around £70k on staff time — originally planned for 3 × G6 over 1 year, now more realistically [[JS]] plus 2 others over 5 months.
