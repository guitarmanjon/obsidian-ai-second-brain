## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Introductions: [[Chris Palser-Thorne]] - account manager for TUoS, local relationship manager for AMRC; [[Matt Bollom]] - offering manager for NI (based in Austin), works with partners on strategy and mutual benefit.
- [[Chris]] recapped the situation leading to the call: switch from tier 1 to tier 2; NI previously saw benefit in the relationship for developing condition monitoring at NI; tier 1 contributions weren't fully used over the years; the simple equipment budget for AMRC staff added little value and little room for development; move to tier 2 intended to allow a better working relationship; the £100k equipment request was a lot bigger than NI expected.
- [[JS]] noted AMRC suggested the move from tier 1 to tier 2.
- [[Chris]]: difficult to determine what AMRC can contribute to NI; NI aren't set up to do InnovateUK-style projects, though they can provide letters of support for EPSRC. Focus for Chris at NI seemed to be all about revenue.
- [[Gav]] (Gavin): two strands of discussion - current equipment proposal and the working relationship; noted continual change of point of contact and focus at NI; confused about what NI actually want from the membership.
- [[Matt]]: talked at length but said little on NI's actual strategy; is planning to visit at some point.
- [[Chris]]: local interest is sales and marketing for NI UK; engineering resource exists to support projects.
- [[Gav]]: framed it as two different relationships - corporate in the US (fairly clear focus on condition monitoring) versus the UK team (unclear, needs more definition). Asked whether the equipment request is a firm no or if there's a middle ground.
- [[Chris]] deferred to [[Matt]], who wanted to understand which groups had asked for equipment - [[Gavin]] explained who they were. [[Chris]] confirmed he has no budget to fund the equipment request. [[Matt]] wanted to understand priorities and what's really needed, not wanting equipment to sit unused on a shelf.
- [[Gav]] pushed for a workable number, and for AMRC to rationalise the equipment list with justification for where the kit will be used and why it's needed.
- [[Matt]] needs a conversation with [[Chris]] about how the relationship moves forward, and is keen to visit AMRC.
