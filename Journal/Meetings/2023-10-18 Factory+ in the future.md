## Attendees

- [[Alex]] - author of the meeting invite notes discussed.

## Purpose

- Discuss the future of Factory+ - both its operational funding/support model and its commercial positioning with external partners.

## Key Discussion Points

- Operational: the Factory+ team is spending increasing amounts of time supporting operational deployment, management, and maintenance of the Factory+ instance, funded creatively rather than through a defined stream. If Factory+ is to keep running operationally across AMRC, a funding stream needs to be defined and/or the work migrated to IT. The alternative is scaling right down to one or two cells for demo purposes only.
- Commercial: a number of partners - namely Boeing, Spirit, and Technicut - are deploying the AMRC Connectivity Stack (ACS) and starting to ask questions about support and commitment to development. AMRC's current answer, per strategy/goals, is that "ACS is not a supported solution; it's a technical demo of how powerful the Factory+ way of thinking is."
- Initial engagement with Razor has been "less than stellar" and it's crunch time - guidance needed on how to position AMRC and ensure an aligned organisational approach.
- Ideally, day-to-day support for Factory+ moves to the AMRC IT team - there is interest from [[Nick Liversidge|Nick]].

## Decisions

- Preferred direction: day-to-day support for Factory+ should move to the AMRC IT team, given interest from [[Nick Liversidge|Nick]].
