## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- [[Richard James]] gave an overview of what AMRC is about (mostly for Mick and Mike's benefit) and discussed how metrology fits in.
- Shop floor tour to show some of the work AMRC does.
- JS presented on PM&C, current projects, and activities.
- Talked through the MBD projects in detail, including issues and challenges encountered.
- [[Mike Wilson]] fetched his laptop from the car to demo PC-DMIS, believing it addresses many of the issues/challenges discussed.
- PC-DMIS overview:
	- PC-DMIS NC is of particular interest (also discussed in the ADG140 literature review).
	- PC-DMIS Gage runs on the controller, for operators to use for quick measurement tasks, with a UI to set up measurements.
	- All PC-DMIS products can link into a central database/server to collect data for a part/series of parts (including via manual gauges).
	- No macros required on the machine (uncertain) - the programming software outputs NC code to run on the machine.
	- Not entirely clear how data gets from the machine to the database, but apparently it can be viewed in real time.
- [[Dave Jeffers]], a PC-DMIS expert, was due in the UK in a couple of weeks to train the UK Hexagon team; Mike said he'd ensure Dave finds time to visit AMRC to discuss the technical aspects of PC-DMIS.
