## Key Discussion Points

- Floor rated at 50kN per square metre, 173mm concrete; NMV3000 machines are 10 tonnes.
- No issues on heavier machines with the current floor - all fine until the latest delivery of NMV3000 (no. 3).
- Substantial cracks found in the floor.
- The machine's original position (near a roller door) was subject to rapid air moves; it was moved to an adjacent slab and issues have lessened but are still present.
- In scope for investigation:
  - Impact on the floor when the machine is cutting.
  - Impact on a finishing machine when heavy roughing is taking place on another machine on the adjacent slab.
  - Validating the response of the machine tool.
- 4 inch cores taken to validate the floor spec - found up to 150mm in places, with sponge insulation underneath the concrete. Not yet tested - results expected in about a month.
- [[Omer Ozkirimli|OO]] estimated 2-3 days on-site time and 2 weeks of off-site analysis, to diagnose where the issue lies.
