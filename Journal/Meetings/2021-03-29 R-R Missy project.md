## Purpose

- To review the [[Rolls-Royce]] [[MISSY]] project (SAMULET 4) and understand its structure, funding, and partners.

## Key Discussion Points

- Project is SAMULET 4.
- Funded via ATI: direct to AMRC from ATI, plus sub-contract from [[Rolls-Royce]].
- 4-year programme, likely launching Q3 2021.
- [[Jamie McGourlay]] (JMcG) gave an overview of the project:
  - Rotatives and Compressors are the internal sponsors at [[Rolls-Royce]].
  - [[Jamie McGourlay]] and [[Donka]] are the [[Rolls-Royce]] leads.
  - MCRL 4 is the target maturity level.
  - Partners: AMRC, [[AFRC]], Hermle (TBC), [[Siemens]] (TBC), a sensor systems integrator (TBC), and an IT solution provider.
  - Expected to be an IP-rich project.
  - Possible opportunity to bring in speech and language tech as potential input/output.
  - Not a fundamental science project - it's about bringing existing technology together to deliver [[MISSY]].
  - Scope includes 350 test pieces.
- Open question: will all (or as much as possible) of the information currently available at [[Rolls-Royce]] be shared with the project? Noted that SAM3 had a few "set us up to fail" situations previously.
