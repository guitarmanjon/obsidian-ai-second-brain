## Purpose

- For [[JS]] to share AMRC's past work retrofitting sensors, and to understand Carpenter's digitalisation journey and needs.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[JS]] gave an overview of AMRC's past work retrofitting sensors, etc.
- [[Towhidul Hoque]] (Tow) on Carpenter's position:
  - Carpenter is still at the incubator phase of digitalisation.
  - Additive is a big part of future revenue; Tow's responsibility is execution of the digitalisation journey.
  - Currently building a backbone for digitalisation, aiming to make full use of data for data-driven capabilities.
  - Legacy systems chain: ERP -> MES -> PLC.
  - Digitisation status: not yet ready to connect legacy PLCs; focus is on data profiling of assets/processes; uncertain about capital investment given asset longevity; current work is tying together data streams/sources (MES, PLCs).
  - Additive is in a better position than Carpenter's 130-year-old traditional business (SAO): lower production volume, data captured via MES/PLC but few sensors, no complete data genealogy.
  - Data is currently collected at the parent level (order ID, PO, etc.); child/process-level data is lacking.
  - "Burning needs" identified:
    - Bring strong data governance and strategy - tidy up the high-level business initiative. No data profiling or data dictionary exists; only front-of-house process people know what the data means.
    - Build an IIoT platform to collect data from source with minimum latency, informing how to digitise operations.
    - Monetise data by building data analytics products.
- Follow-on call planned: Tow to share architecture; discuss challenges with very old machines, an inventory of machines, and new machines as well.

## Decisions

- Agreed to hold a follow-on call to go deeper on architecture and machine inventory.
