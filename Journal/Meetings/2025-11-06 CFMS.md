## Purpose

- Meeting between AMRC and CFMS to realign the two organisations and explore collaboration opportunities.

## Key Discussion Points

- Introductions all round.
- Background context: prior collaboration with CFMS on the Airlift project.
- CFMS reached out to [[Andrew Silcox|Andy Silcox]] to try to realign the two organisations.
- CFMS capabilities: robotics is now a focus area for CFMS; IoT is no longer a focus; compute capability (CPUs and GPUs) is available for people to start experimenting on.
- CFMS have been in discussion with [[Rob Weatherhead]] and another (unnamed) contact regarding ATI programmes.
- [[Siva]] gave an overview of NW activities, including training AI models to capture tacit knowledge.
- CFMS have the compute for heavy AI workloads, but need high-speed connectivity examples.
- COSDAC (collaborative space data centre) - a sandbox environment for testing cloud-to-edge connectivity.
- Robot simulation: a large AI simulator referred to as "Larasim" (name uncertain in source) - positioned as a competitor to Omniverse but not constrained by license fees, and supports multiple robots in the same virtual space; possible link to COMPASS. A data centre was needed to support this, which is why CFMS have been developing their own data centre solution.
- Worth CFMS engaging with the wider HVMC as well.
- CFMS's factory optimisation tools are well advanced, allowing development of fairly novel layouts and simulation of robotics and machine tools.
