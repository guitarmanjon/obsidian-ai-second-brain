## Attendees

- [[JS]]
- [[Steve McVey]]
- [[Andy Spalding]]
- [[Pete Willoughby]]

## Purpose

- Discuss sensor data capture on the machine, MTT's position on future collaboration, and scope a roadmap.

## Key Discussion Points

- Primarily about sensor data capture - [[JS]] gave an overview of the current setup and desired future state.
- [[Pete Willoughby]]: MTT did the refurb as a means to an end, i.e. getting the data. [[Pete Willoughby]] thinks AMRC want to go it alone; [[JS]] said this is not the case. [[Pete Willoughby]] said MTT need to see the bigger picture before committing to short-term work. Proposed a scoping session where each side independently considers what they want to achieve short and long term, then reconvenes to discuss and develop a roadmap.
- MTT have stopped industrialising retrofitted sensors on machines - OEMs are catching up / taking over in this area.
- Development of databasing - joint venture, or MTT to develop alone? If MTT lack the skills, they may go and find external resource. [[Steve McVey]]: there are technical reasons why neither side could go it alone - collaboration is probably the fastest route. Learning from the [[5G]] work is to be applied to Full Monty.
- [[5G]]: security is a big part of it, with [[BAE Systems]] particularly interested. However, MTT's planned [[5G]] work isn't necessarily novel - it's just using [[5G]]. Adaptive control is a key focus for MTT going forward.
- [[Sinumerik]] Edge: MTT working with [[Siemens]] on [[Sinumerik]] One - talking to all levels of the business, a high profile application. Digital twin work in the [[Sinumerik]] world - unclear if MTT will do all digital twin work with [[Siemens]]. Machine design also included in this work. Legacy of tools for maintenance.
- MTT have moved to TRL 6-9 since AMRC last spoke with them.
- Erlangen - has opened a door to them; discussion around developer info for proprietary IP from MTT and [[University of Huddersfield|Huddersfield]].

## Decisions

- None recorded - a scoping session was proposed but not yet scheduled.
