## Attendees

- [[JS]], [[GH]], [[AB]], [[Jack Rooke]], [[HB]]

## Purpose

- To align the group on what ATR currently covers, how it fits into the wider grant vision and AMRC work-area landscape, and to review a proposed ATR cell design with Mills CNC.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[GH]] asked what ATR covers today; [[AB]] described it as a **data-centric manufacturing environment** built on interoperability of equipment and software, automation (IMG cells, AGVs, robots, scanners, metrology), and "getting the data for TMG" to support better process planning.
- [[Jack Rooke]] presented a simplified maturity model for the grant vision - a table running from Level 0 (Fully Manual) to Level 5 (Future Autonomy) across a Physical strand and a Digital strand, benchmarked against typical SME, advanced OEM, and grant TRL tiers - framed around the question of how to help manufacturers move to a higher maturity level. Grants are being targeted to fund this progression.
- A work-area flow chart (colour-coded by AMRC department/group) was reviewed, showing how several work streams feed into TMG: VM Model development (Technology Team), Data Centric Manufacturing (Technology Team + IMG), INSTI Wireless adaption (Technology/Process Teams + IMG), and Carbon Calculator (Technology Team).
- Within the "UoS Department / AMRC Group" box, the chart showed: Cognitive Task Analysis and Liger Integration both feeding into the School of EEE; School of EEE feeding into **ATR**; TMG and NW linked bidirectionally with **ATR**; 5G Connectivity feeding into NW; and **ATR** linked bidirectionally with **IMG**. IMG in turn connects to Interoperability/Apollo Protocol, Robotic Integrations, Digital Passport, and Digital Thread.
- Question raised about who makes up the DCM (Data Centric Manufacturing) team within TMG.
- Jon Eyre identified as the contact for Interoperability / Apollo Protocol work.
- [[AB]] noted the intent to get mature, ready technology deployed into ATR.
- An ATR Cell proposal was shown, developed in collaboration with **Mills CNC** - a 3D-rendered manufacturing cell comprising a Light Gate (for autonomous mobile robot integration), two machine tools (a Mill-turn or Vertical 5-axis, and a Vertical 3-Axis), a 7-axis Robot, a Control Station, a Metrology Station, and tool/part/vice/pallet loading capability.
- [[AB]] flagged that CAM is "woefully behind" in the data space, raising the need for autonomy for CAM. Data Centric Manufacturing was reiterated as a recurring theme, alongside the challenge of many different, unconsolidated databases across AMRC that need unifying.
- Under "Data Centric Manufacturing," a note flagged potential fellowship work here for [[Lindsay Lee]].
- Databasing for ATR is currently built around Enza's work with RSE and MISSY.

## Decisions

- No firm decision recorded. Floated: targeting grants to advance the maturity-model vision; unifying AMRC's disparate databases under Data Centric Manufacturing; possible fellowship work for [[Lindsay Lee]] in this space.
