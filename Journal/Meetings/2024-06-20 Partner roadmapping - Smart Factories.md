## Attendees

- [[James L]] ([[BAE Systems|BAE]])
- [[Jamie McGourlay|Jamie]] ([[Rolls-Royce|R-R]])

## Purpose

- Partner roadmapping session for the Smart Factories theme, gathering feedback on the DS and [[AI]] roadmap.

## Key Discussion Points

- Use of [[LLM|LLMs]] as part of a research project - [[James L]] @ [[BAE Systems|BAE]]: [[BAE Systems|BAE]] have updated their T&Cs to include the use of [[LLM|LLMs]] for content generation. On the whole they are OK with it but want transparency in its use, including prompts/outputs in an appendix and details of how [[LLM]] output was scrutinised and fact-checked. Highlighted potential time savings on tech scans/research scans, with that time better spent on risky implementation of interesting technology. First stage work could be to see how much time is actually saved using an [[LLM]], given the scrutiny needed on its output.
- Data Assurance - [[Jamie McGourlay|Jamie]] @ [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]]: proving the quality of data at a basic level - do we know what the basics are, have we met them, and are there new standards we should be aware of.
- Cost of [data/[[AI]]] - [[Jamie McGourlay|Jamie]]: cost of using data and [[AI]] tools, now and in future, and the cost of not doing it. Example given of costs highlighted in [[MISSY]] being much higher than Jamie expected (project name not mentioned).
- [[AI]] advising [[AI]] - [[James L]] @ [[BAE Systems|BAE]]: using an [[AI]] system to look across an existing data and [[AI]] pipeline, assess it, and suggest other models or methods that could be tried.
- Green [[AI]] - [[James L]]: [[BAE Systems|BAE]] recently found that [[Azure]] provides CO2e data for cloud-based compute; this is now part of [[BAE Systems|BAE]]'s [[Power BI|PowerBI]] reporting, above financial cost. Also worth considering the compute required to compile code - "Python is the dirtiest of the lot."
- Total cost of ownership for data - [[Jamie McGourlay|Jamie]]: arose from discussion around "low cost" in relation to data capture from Hatim's PM&C presentation. Accepted that low-cost refers to the hardware to capture data at the machine edge, but the whole solution soon becomes high cost because of the systems that data capture tools/hardware/methods need to interact with, the security restrictions around these, and the cost of implementing the whole data pipeline. Also true for SMEs if they must abide by the rules of companies they supply/are supplied by.
