## Purpose

> [!info] Why did this meeting happen?
> Workshop with NAMRC to explore Industry 4.0 sensing/monitoring opportunities across Sellafield's nuclear waste vitrification processes.

- Identify condition-monitoring and sensing challenges around the vitrification test rig (VTR) and waste vitrification pipeline (WVP) that AMRC expertise could address.

## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Background: vitrification is the removal of highly active nuclear waste; VTR = vitrification test rig; WVP = waste vitrification pipeline.
- Calciner roller bearing deterioration (VTR):
  - Thermal ("hedronic") distortion due to operating conditions; temperature range 100-700degC.
  - Could look at using an inductive sensor with an analogue output instead of the current binary output.
  - Vibration sensing/fingerprinting, similar to the approach used for machine tools.
  - Distortion of the roller currently unknown, likely microns ([[Andy Cooney]]).
- Calciner in-leakage, source and location (VTR & WVP): system is under negative pressure, so in-leakage can occur; frequency response of the system is relevant.
- Scrubber blockages (WVP): possible vibration analysis approach.
- Off-gas deterioration (VTR & WVP): closely related to the calciner in-leakage issue.
