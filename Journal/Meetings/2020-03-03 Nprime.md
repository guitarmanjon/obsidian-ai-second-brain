## Attendees

- AMRC: [[JS]], [[Omer Ozkirimli|OO]], [[Alan Lyons]]
- Nprime: [[Ciaran O'Shea]] (MD), [[Syed Mahmoud]]

## Purpose

- Introductory meeting with Nprime to explore their vibration/data-capture sensor technology and a potential servitised monitoring model.

## Key Discussion Points

- DTS data capture device: 3 channel, 5MHz per channel, very small form factor (1x1x0.5").
- Discussed wireless telemetry systems and tuned piezo-electric cells for vibration sensing.
- Nprime driving towards a servitised model - approx. £1k system cost, plus a monthly cost for acquisition and cloud analytics.
- Off-the-shelf devices currently used at AMRC - open question on whether to move towards customised devices.
- Latte Panda and "Hertzian response" mentioned.
- Capturing and manipulating good, correct data is described as Nprime's speciality.
