## Purpose

- First in a series of regular catch-ups with [[Rikki]] to get an overview of the current portfolio of projects, understand the team from Rikki's perspective, and establish whether a roadmap/strategy exists for the theme.

## Key Discussion Points

- Three areas of the theme: Factory+/connectivity, data science, and AI/computer vision. Overall aim is to streamline the digital thread pipeline, standardise onboarding of devices so they appear the same regardless of programming, and overcome barriers to AI adoption. [[Bikram]] covers ML ops, [[Lindsey]] covers traceability of AI systems. [[Rab]] and [[Ben Morrow|Ben]] have asked for a strategy.
- Data science crew: [[Lindsey]] leading (statistician, has unearthed problems with prior work, particularly vision; good links to Leeds and Sheffield maths/stats depts; has some funding from previous academic work). [[Tim]] - known quantity. [[Leo]] - "dark horse".
- Smart factory: [[Artur Grigals|Artur]] (ex-Tribosonics, LabVIEW expert but not a fan) and [[Edwin]] (robotics, reinforcement learning).
- Cybersecurity: a bit of a capability hole; need to catch up with [[Harry Burroughes|Harry]] on this.
- Machine vision and AI - [[Narcisa]]: visual inspection has moved to [[Jaymes Truman]] (a different theme); machine vision is more about whether the computer understands what it's seeing. [[Bikram]] is tech lead for AI and does a lot of machine vision. [[Kevin Obuya|Kevin]] covers visual inspection. [[Grace Lim]] covers AI and cyber, leaning towards visual. [[Wei Kin]] is a jack-of-all-trades across Unity and AI.
- [[Alex]] (Tech Fellow) has admitted he doesn't have much input into AI.
- SRDS: generally a happy team. [[Lindsay]] is at the top of G7, being considered for a Tech Fellow role, potentially for AI.
- Projects: ISCF with R-R (visual inspection of blades) - looking to make it more robust and explain its decisions (I2C2 - image interclass classification coefficient); involves AV&R; end goal is to state what a defect is with confidence and reasoning, while a human still decides how bad the defect is. [[James Fortune]] - [[Rikki]] will introduce [[JS]]. Commercial work outside R-R is currently low; lots of Catapult work but not enough promotion - first job is to get some articles out. Also mentioned: Anglo-American, RAVINE ([[Narcisa]] and [[Bikram]] - AML), and Catapult for AI (synthetic data for PPE on building sites).
- Pipeline: [[Lindsay]], [[Tim]], [[Leo]], [[Bikram]] form a cross-AMRC working group (a "digi champions" type initiative). LongOps - targeting low TRL, a Manchester and Reading consortium, light-touch bridge-building.
- Factory+: SME engagement planned; data models/management needed work, setting back plans; next year Catapult money will fund engagement with local firms and tier 1s, working towards production-ready; the following year targets ADG, ABG, AS as a product for integrators; pushing infrastructure work (software imaging, gateways) over to IT this year to free up [[Alex]] and team for research; not much to commercialise around Redhat; cloud connectivity is longer term; feedback from [[Dave Richards]] on architecture.
