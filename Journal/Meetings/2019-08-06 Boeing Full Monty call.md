## Purpose

## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- [[Ryan Hanks]]: [[Rui Moreira]] proposed a project highly related to the Full Monty ABG work.
- [[Rui Moreira]]:
  - Wants to learn more about IO-Link technology.
  - Noted prior issues around voltage differences, connectors, and operators having to manually start up systems.
  - Has a Statement of Work for a project looking into IO-Link, and asked about scope to include IO-Link in Full Monty - likely not feasible at this point.
  - [[Matt F]] agreed with that assessment and asked about other platforms - Hermle noted as a possibility - and whether Boeing have target machines; [[Rui Moreira]] said he really just wants to test the system and understand how it performs.
  - [[Rui Moreira]] wants to tag onto an existing project to save on overheads - possibly piggy-backing on Full Monty. Question raised on how to pay for IO-Link kit; [[Matt F]] suggested it might be possible to fund through an ADG bolt-on.
  - [[Rui Moreira]] has contacts at Siemens who can assist, also has an IO-Link master, and has been in touch with the ifm CEO, who is keen to support.
  - [[Rui Moreira]] will send documentation from the call for review, along with Siemens and ifm contact details.
