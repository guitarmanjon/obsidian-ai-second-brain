## Key Discussion Points

- [[Mark Coombs|Mark Coombes]] - 30 years at Siemens across Service and Applications, now runs the Machine Tool Business in the UK, also has a vertical role in Power and Energy.
- SMR has already been discussed with [[Mark Coombs|Mark Coombes]].
- Expansion of the SMR demo at AMRC to Aero customers (R-R, GKN, etc.) - potential UK demonstrator for the Teamcentre stack etc.
- Getting output of Teamcentre to the shop floor is the current gap - this lends itself quite well to Sinumerik One.
- [[Mark Coombs|Mark Coombes]] needs information to take back to the R&D budget: how would AMRC implement it, what would the use case look like, etc.
- Potential to get AMRC a Sinumerik One - would be the NCU itself, needing retrofit to a machine; might be more time-consuming and trouble than it's worth.
- Digital modelling is the core of this work and can still be used on 840D controllers; latest 840D software has improvements comparable to some Sinumerik One capability.
- VNCK - precursor to Model My Machine.
- Virtualisation is very important to R-R.
- Brownfield Connector - Siemens server-based software for connectivity across many different controllers/assets.
- Discussion of setting up a Hub for Siemens tech and demonstrators, with funds to support setup/development of projects; possible Connected Factory in SFIH.
- Workshop planned in Sheffield: 2 days covering the latest technology - Industrial Edge, Sinumerik Edge, Sinumerik One, Brownfield Connectivity, and choosing the right kit for the job.
