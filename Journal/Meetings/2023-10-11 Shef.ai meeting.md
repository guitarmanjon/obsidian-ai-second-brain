## Purpose

- Shef.ai event, including a panel session on Sheffield's AI/data opportunities and CMI investment priorities.

## Key Discussion Points

- Panel Q1: How can Sheffield better embrace newly arising opportunities such as the new Civil Service Hub, Investment Zone, and Digital Health Hub?
  - AMRC is placed at the centre of the Investment Zone announcement.
  - A key part of the funding is the COMPASS work, including a new facility with the latest automation technology for producing large carbon fibre structures.
  - The data opportunities are massive - the new facility will be fully connected, with all data-generating assets subscribed and their data available.
  - Potential datasets from this new facility could be exactly what manufacturing research needs - high-quality datasets from actual production processes, free from IP and sensitivity restrictions - making future CMI work opportunities huge.
  - The Digital Health Hub could provide important learning for AMRC as it seeks to provide a data hub for manufacturing research data.
- Panel Q2: What are the critical gaps and priority areas that the CMI investment can address?
  - Excitement that AI for Manufacturing is one of the CMI themes.
  - Personal perspective: education and training for industry is key - a lot of industry doesn't really know how data science and AI can benefit them, or even what these things are.
  - Two levels of training needed: senior management understanding/buy-in, and training engineers on the ground with at least some data literacy to enable intelligent conversations.
  - Ideal data workflow pairs a data expert (data researcher/scientist) with a domain expert (e.g. a machining expert who understands what peaks and troughs in a signal mean).
  - Training doesn't need to focus on "how" (e.g. programming in Jupyter notebooks) - it needs to focus on the "why" and top-level meaning of the tools and processes.
  - LLMs have generated a lot of interest in AI and "thought leadership" from firms like Gartner, Deloitte, Accenture, etc. - but SME manufacturers need guidance on actual value and practical first steps.
