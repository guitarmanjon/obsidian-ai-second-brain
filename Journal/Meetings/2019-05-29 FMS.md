## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Independent Coolant Monitoring Solution presented:
  - Battery-operated sensors running 24/7.
  - Basic system covers coolant level and monitoring of consumption.
  - Manual input to app for pH, concentration, appearance, comments, etc.
    - pH and concentration are not automated because sensors for these typically need regular cleaning and recalibration.
    - Automated pH capture possible via RGB capture of litmus paper with a constant light source.
  - Supports operators doing checks and updates.
    - Also fits with a servitised model - FMS do the maintenance but have the data before they arrive on site.
