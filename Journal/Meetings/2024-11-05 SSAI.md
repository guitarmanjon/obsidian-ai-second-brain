## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Purpose 1: introduce two software libraries SSAI are building towards AI for enhancing design/manufacturing optimisation - "Physics with AI" and "AI Enhanced Design Manufacturing Optimization".
  - Focus for SSAI is AI-enabled design, manufacture and optimisation.
  - Knowledge sharing across groups is difficult, particularly with defence/official secrets constraints.
  - [[JS]] gave an overview of AMRC's data-sharing challenges.
  - Semiconductor manufacturing industry is very reluctant to share - recipes for making semiconductors are highly secretive, though individual generic processes (not the overall recipe) might be shareable - likened to feature-based machining, where individual features are done in generic well-known ways but the parts/overall process remain proprietary.
  - Discussion on how to put a "trade secret" around learning - LLMs perhaps making progress towards this.
- Purpose 2: get feedback on open-sourcing the above libraries and creating a site for researchers/academics to use the libraries, share data, equations, papers, etc. - essentially a community for openly showing how AI is used in manufacturing and why.
  - SSAI considering a GitHub/Hugging-Face-style site for sharing models, data, and published work.
  - Concern that young people are not entering the semiconductor industry - a 15-year training path to become a fab engineer; facilities are being built but the workforce doesn't exist.
  - [[JS]]'s interest is in sharing of AMRC data; [[Abhi Rampall]]'s focus is on physics-based models.
  - Question raised: why share all the AMRC data? Need to answer this. Two possible approaches - put data out there and see who comes (advertise it), or put data plus models out there and see who bites.
  - Noted there's nothing out there that's manufacturing-specific for data/models; referenced the Fraunhofer list of datasets (https://www.bigdata-ai.fraunhofer.de/s/datasets/index.html).
  - Collaboration aspect of the proposed site is key - how to take the work/data further.
  - Physics constraints on AI models discussed.
  - Target audience for the website is manufacturers, not necessarily AI/data specialists.
  - Why SSAI are going to do this: massive need from the semiconductor world; [[Abhi Rampall]] finds it "pathetic" there's no central manufacturing database given how much manufacturing goes on; they have a business model for the idea's longevity.
