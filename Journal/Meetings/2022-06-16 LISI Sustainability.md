## Purpose

- Capture LISI's changing approach to performance metrics, tooling efficiency, and energy/compressed air use.

## Key Discussion Points

- Shift in mindset for performance metrics: 60,000 parts/day target used to be the sole focus; now teams are also interested in hitting that target with reduced consumable tooling.
- Comparison of 2,000 vs 12,000 broach pin life: fewer tool changes means easier work for operators, fewer consumables, less setting and settling-in time.
- Electricity and compressed air use: previously machines were left cycling even without parts going through.
- Factory-level tracking of electricity usage against output (kg produced) and compressed air usage led to an improvement project to find leaks etc.
- Cost to make broach pins reduced from £60,000 to £15,000.
- Tool regrind is now used much more.
