## Purpose

## Key Discussion Points

### CHESCO overview ([[Heiko Witte|Heiko]])
- Site is in a former coal-mining area, so land re-use was a design consideration.
- CHESCO GmbH is set up as a limited company, a 100% subsidiary, but has its own direction/finance -> more agility, less admin.
- TRL 5-6 target, with a full digitalized cycle of design -> production -> test -> analysis.
- LuFo funding is similar in nature to ATI funding.
- Three cross-cutting themes: Thermal management; Efficient Manufacturing Tech.; Digital Tech. (cyber also a consideration).
- Industrial partners include some large, local companies (e.g. an MD aero regional aircraft manufacturer).
- An altitude test bed is being set up for testing hybrid-electric aero engines.
- [[GH]] asked about areas of strength and collaboration - **automation** and lots of areas of overlap between CHESCO and AMRC were identified.
- Funding is mostly LuFo, including an RL project with Deutsche Bahn; there is a 20% limit on industry use of equipment due to funding conditions.
- Close link with **BTU** - CHESCO pulling some people along with it.
- ISO certs (9001 & 9100): standards are sometimes relaxed for research-focused projects not resulting in an aero part, though the facility itself still has to meet the given standards. CHESCO noted they are still learning in this area.

### Halle 4 tour - inspection & AI
- CT machines for aero part inspection, including in Inconels (Shandara).
- Automated inspection of ball bearings for Schaeffler Aero, including ceramic ball inspection.
- AI models for detecting cracks -> **put in touch with [[NP]]**.
  - Data availability is a challenge; synthetic data generation engineering is being explored.
- Knowledge transfer topics: ceramic additive manufacturing; R-R electric engine wear -> using Microsoft Loop for research capture.

### Halle 5 tour - additive manufacturing ([[Angelika]] + Mark)
- Additive manufacturing facility focused on process knowledge development and digitalisation.
  - Machinery sourced from a previous funding round.
  - AGILE project is aimed at learning about the process.
  - Digital twinning of the whole process chain.
- Ceramic AM being used for a rotor casing.
- Big focus on connectivity of machines for digital twinning - real-time data feeds for researchers, remote access to data.
- Noted idea: 1000 Kelvin - AI for design of AM parts?
- Vacuum furnace for heat treat and quench of AM parts.
- End-to-end data capture across the whole AM process.

### Machining/Halle tour ([[Stephan Weiss|Stephan]])
- Sub-halle featuring a variety of 5-axis Chiron machines.
- Work spans geometry, material and process forms.
- Predictive Line Simulation on the machine, using machine code (Chiron product) - crash protection via prediction.
- Potential link-up to [[Jack Rooke]] for AI for CAM.
- Feedback loop using a BLUM laser for tool measurement.
- Zoller tool measurement uses barcode scanning to link tool data into the machine tools.
- SINUMERIK One controllers fitted on the Chiron machines.

### "Lopper" electric motor project ([[Lam Nguyen]])
- Collaboration with MD Aircraft, Additive Drives GmbH, [[BTU]] and Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft.
- Developing a motor based on the operational requirements of a plane (10 people, max 15 kft, no icing, regional, < 450 km/h).
  - Operational profiles: (1) Max TOW, (2) Emergency (bursts of high power / power-time profiles), (3) Ferry (standard operation, constant low power).
- Assembly process:
  - Robotic winding to handle flexibility/variability.
  - Modular and reconfigurable design.
  - **Data is core** - capture, storage, analysis.
  - In-situ quality control.
