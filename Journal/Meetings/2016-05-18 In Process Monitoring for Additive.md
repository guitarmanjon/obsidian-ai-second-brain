## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- (Source text was two interleaved columns - a meeting agenda/notes column and a pasted email from Abdul to James; disentangled below.)
- Agenda: overview of the Innovate UK call (connected digital additive manufacturing); ideas for industrial lead partners to invite; who will contact these partners. 6th June agenda: intro to InnoUK call; what we're doing at the moment (NAMTEC, Design, FoF); each partner to present 5 mins on what they're up to; discussion of what the project could be.
- Data driven manufacturing for additive: capture data during the additive process to monitor a part throughout its life; use this data to influence further processing (e.g. adjust cutting parameters based on additive output); could monitor surface finish/form using something like Google Glass (VR element).
- Process monitoring ideas: sensors in the machine to monitor temperature (especially thin-walled sections that would benefit from lower temp/shorter laser exposure), air temp, gas purity/particulates; optical monitoring of each layer to recognise an incorrect layer and repair or stop the process; potential partners Moog, Renishaw, MSP - where's the innovation potential, sensors on the AM machine to influence process, data travelling with a component through its life. Residual stress flagged as an issue with Renishaw machines; possible process modelling. Optical measurement - using laser optics for other purposes.
- James Hughes not available that week - Kristina to stand in.
- Contacts identified: Ian Todd (Materials group) - has a 125mm machine already modded, could be modded further; James Hunt - ST trying to poach him for a Sector Lead role in additive; James Hughes to contact. Renishaw - TM already contacted Mat Favell. NAMRC - Mark d-souza Mathew. Abdul Haq (Medical). Abdulha - PhD student supervised by James/Keith, looking at additive. Material suppliers - NAMTEC has contacts, plus Carpenter and Marr. GKN - in next week to discuss bulk additive. Also MTC and AML.
- Email from Abdul (forwarded, re the same meeting): apologised he may not make the meeting in FOF due to a Sellafield Ltd meeting overrunning; offered written comments on the agenda questions instead. Suggested Renishaw be involved, particularly people from their software/design/development teams, noting they're already working on in-process monitoring for additive though no capability is on their existing machines yet; suggested a software partner such as Autodesk would be useful (unsure if they're already partners) since a usable platform is needed to turn monitoring data back into design input for the AM process. Abdul's view: the project should be aimed at improving powder-bed metal additive manufacture, specifically:
	- Monitoring residual stress during the build and intelligently adjusting laser parameters to suit geometry.
	- Monitoring wiper blade condition during the build.
	- Monitoring powder layer thickness applied to the substrate.
	- Monitoring airborne particulate levels in the build chamber (affects build quality).
	- Monitoring argon gas flow/path during a build and optimising for uniform cooling.
	- Monitoring thermal conditions in part, powder, build substrate, and melt pool.
	- Monitoring laser conditions to validate that software-level settings are actually applied during the build.
	- A self-calibrating machine that knows when conditions are out of limits and either self-adjusts or flags that a service is required.
	- Abdul felt the project would be better placed with involvement from AMRC's design and machining groups.
