- SolidStateAI is a Canadian software company involved.
- Two proposal themes: (1) Research - AML looking at how to link all data across their shop floor, possibly using AI; needs a UK company and university plus a Canadian company and NRC; (2) Productivity.

- Submission deadline 9th May; requires J-eS form and costs from AMRC.
- Project must start by 1st October, running for 2 years. AMRC's time involvement is minimal - seen as a favour and a good opportunity for relationship building.
- Budget: max for UK consortium is £200k; Gareth will want at least £150k; AMRC could do 2 periods of £25k - TF only, with PM overhead, JS and AB.
