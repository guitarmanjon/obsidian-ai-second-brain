## Attendees

- [[JS]], [[Haiping]], [[Helen Kennedy|Helen K]], [[Nikki Dibben]], [[Guy Brown]], [[George Panoutsos]], [[Saul Cozens]]

## Purpose

- CMI Advisory Board meeting covering sector strategy, director recruitment, RAI submission outcomes, and Theme Lead/Fellow allocation.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[George Panoutsos]] (GP) gave feedback on sectors: why [[AI]] for Manufacturing when there's already so much [[AI]] for Engineering, etc.
- Director recruitment: an offer has been made to a candidate, but it's not being announced yet due to the candidate's ongoing relationship with their current employer.
- RAI submissions: two Expressions of Interest (EoIs) were submitted, neither successful.
- Theme Lead and Fellow applications:
  - GP in favour of spreading Theme Leads (TLs) and Fellows out, rather than bunching them into one area.
  - [[Saul Cozens]] (SC) suggested working out which TLs need a Fellow.
  - GP concerned that some applications lacked depth in [[AI]].
  - [[JS]] raised the question of whether a Fellow should go into a department/project without strong [[AI]] leadership, or whether a TL is needed there first to support the Fellow.
  - [[Helen Kennedy|HK]] asked what Sheffield wants to be known for in [[AI]], and argued this should influence where the internal investment is directed.
  - Proposal for focus areas discussed:
    - Human-Centric [[AI]] - equality/inequality focus.
    - [[AI]]-powered research - Healthcare focus.
    - Robotics & Autonomous Systems - chair proposed; get input from robotics and autonomous systems groups on priorities.
    - [[AI]] in Materials - question of whether Engineering should fund this, though GP thinks there isn't much money in that pot; open question of whether this fits under [[AI]] for Manufacturing, or whether "[[AI]] in Engineering" should be a cover-all title, and whether AMRC would still fund it.

## Decisions

- Offer made to preferred Director candidate; announcement to be held back for now due to the candidate's current employer relationship.

## Action Items

- [[Saul Cozens]] - Work out which Theme Leads need a Fellow.
