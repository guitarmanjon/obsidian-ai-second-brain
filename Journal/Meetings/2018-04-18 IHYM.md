## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- [[KM]] sees links to Servitisation and MP2.3.
- 3 demo geometries planned by end of project, industrially relevant.
- USFD (University of Sheffield) includes AMRC and Namtec.
- Already lots of sensor data available.
- Desirable sensing: hopper temperature and humidity for contaminant detection (online system) - powder baked for 2 hours to remove humidity, rests for 1 hour to cool; over a weekend humidity might exceed acceptable levels. Also oxygen monitoring close to the deposition head.
- Will need to consider a wireless system for in-process monitoring at the workpiece, given the C axis rotates (100-200 rotations).
- Workpiece sensing: temperature of base and fixture; oxygen level around the deposition head.
- Machine data/process collection: MTConnect discussion with DMG Mori; vibration data plus chatter analysis.
- Requirements needed: sensors required, data presentation, data analysis, data storage, statement of requirements, time frame, equipment budget.
- Time frame: WP2 starts May 2018, runs to April 2019; PM&C work is front-end, to be completed by September 2018.
- Nice to have a kit at the end of the project that can be reused on the machine for future work.
