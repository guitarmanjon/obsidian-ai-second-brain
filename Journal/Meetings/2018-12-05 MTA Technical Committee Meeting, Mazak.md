- [[Sam Turner]] (HVMC) - Technology Strategy for HVMC:
	- Strategic objectives & strategy teams: "Increase the uptake of digital and automation technologies to increase UK productivity and global competitiveness."
	- Through-life services strategy team - Servitisation - should the project link up with members of this team?
	- Technology map presented as a tree - branches are tech areas, roots are supporting organisations/teams/people/resources. 2 branches to digi man: data capture, management, analysis; and digital twin.
	- Industrial Strategy Challenge Fund Wave 3:
		- Stephenson challenge: power electronics, motors and drives across 7 sectors (motor, power, on-board ancillaries, chargers, aero, marine, industrial and energy). Building £5bn more is target. £78 million of funding announced.
		- Made Smarter: digital manufacturing - becoming a global leader in industrial digitalisation by 2030. One outcome is to make clear to the global community what the UK's vision is. Adoption is a challenge, particularly for SMEs - not sure where to get started in digitalisation. Accenture report (2017) identified 7 key barriers to adoption - cyber security came out top. NW Pilot is a good start. IDT advisors?
		- Quantum - quantum devices.
- Umati - [[Alexander Broos]], VDW (German machine tool builders association):
	- Universal machine tool interface. Parallels made with USB - why doesn't this exist for machine tools?
	- Common language across all machine tool platforms for connectivity to MES, ERP, etc. Based on the OPC UA standard.
	- Umati supports specific extensions for manufacturers and customers of machine tools. Targeting future machines only - what about legacy?
	- OPC UA is based on defining Use Cases before designing how OPC UA will be applied and what parameters are required (?). Umati has defined 100+ parameters for use cases they have defined.
	- Vertical integration only - not machine to machine, or within machine. OPC UA defines how to communicate, not what - this is where Umati comes in, as an OPC UA Companion Specification defining what data is required by a machine tool to communicate vertically.
	- Companion Spec to be published in 2019. Demo at EMO Hannover, September 2019.
	- Q: How does it compare to MTConnect? Idea behind both is the same. Umati working closely with AMT (MTConnect "owners").
- 5G for Manufacturing - [[Dritan Kaleshi]], Head of Tech for 5G at Digital Catapult:
	- 5G is a step-change in mobile network development - previously infrastructure has been fairly invisible to consumers. Mobile industry now needs to support industry communications (e.g. massive IoT), not just traditional human-to-human customers.
	- Manufacturing is a huge opportunity for 5G - report by Ericsson and Arthur D Little, June 2018, on 5G addressable revenues per industry.
	- Nissan Digital have been involved in a "Made in 5G" use case: connected intelligent machines, connecting the workers, supply chain tracking and monitoring, autonomous logistics.
	- Digital Catapult are proposing that 5G is the best digitalisation enabler.
	- Use cases: Nokia Cooperative Cloud Robots - 5G demo video on YouTube (ABB and Nokia demo); 5G connected quality control; see Ericsson website - "ultra-low latency of 5G improves production of jet engine components".
