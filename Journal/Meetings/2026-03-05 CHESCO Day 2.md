## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- **Medizin University Lausitz - Carl Thiem**
  - Hosted/presented by [[Michael Mallach]] (Head of D&I) and [[Große Meininghaus|Prof. Große Meininghaus]] (catheter ablation).
  - MUL-CT is only 2 years old.
  - Structured around Teaching, Care, Research & 'System + Future Tasks', with digitalisation underpinning all areas and the 'Model health region Lausitz' initiative spanning all areas too.
  - Research focus areas: Digitalisation in Healthcare, and the Health System.
  - German healthcare systems are fragmented, including health sectors and data — raised the question of a "Data Connect" approach.

- **Cardiology - catheter ablation ([[Große Meininghaus|Dr Große Meininghaus]])**
  - Catheter ablation of atrial fibrillation discussed.
  - Previous/current treatment used either heat or cold (+70 or -70) to destroy tissue causing arrhythmia.
  - New method uses a catheter delivering electric shocks to cells (1.5-2kV), with nanosecond delivery killing cells locally.

- **Brain Machine Interface ([[Ehab Shiban]])**
  - Non-, semi- and fully invasive solutions discussed.
  - Used to restore language, hearing, movement, sight — but not always possible.
  - PRIME solution referenced (Elon Musk / Neuralink) — implant allows patients to learn how to control external devices.
  - Pioneering work done in Munich, hopefully coming to Cottbus — [[Ehab Shiban]] is ex-Munich; implant of "Utah Array" mentioned.
  - [[Marcel Ermoff]] at STH is a close colleague of [[Ehab Shiban]].

- **Newton Flight Lab - "Digital Excellence @ CHESCO"**
  - Presented by [[Dirk]] and [[Magda]] (PPL).
  - Areas: digital thread, digital twin + simulation; data & IT infrastructure & architectures; automation & I4.0 & AI; cybersecurity + resilience.
  - CHESCO have their own IT team.
  - **A lot** of overlap in areas of interest with AMRC.
  - Collaboration platform - membership allowing external collaborators to access CHESCO facility securely and safely.
  - Digital Platform = Data Strategy, Systems engineering, interoperability, cybersecurity, & digital sovereignty.
  - DIRECT project: end-to-end digital twin supporting the whole product life cycle, with many 'planned' items around AI (possibly tied to data strategy). CHESCO uses Teamcenter for PLM operationally, alongside NVIDIA Omniverse.
  - **Asset Administration Shell** - standardised data interfaces for digital thread & connectivity between partners and OEMs, covering data exchange systems and data spaces.
  - AR/VR: data transfer challenges — needs to be fast, scalable and robust.
  - 3D printing data feedback: linking process data to CT scan results and feeding this back into process & part design, with verification of parts by data.
  - Security work package: ISO27001 and secure data sharing.

- **DIRECT - Sustainable Aviation Ecosystem ([[Magda]])**
  - Holistic view including aircraft, airport, operators, etc.
  - Work with LareSparx GmbH, leveraging an operational model.
  - Aircraft model of ATR42-600.
  - Battery model: individual cells modeled from manufacturer parameters; various scenarios (time on ground, temperature, charging cycles, battery health) to support certification; consideration of what to do with a battery once it reaches end of life (e.g. 70% max capacity).
  - Propulsion system model: CS23 used as a case study, with Simulink modelling.
  - From propulsion system model to airframe design: airframe designed around the propulsion system, using an open (BSD?) NASA open source design tool.

- **D4M Project**
  - With Deutsche Bahn and maintenance operators.
  - RL-based AI model for weekly maintenance planning.
  - Integration of data from DB & Fraunhofer.
  - Balancing resources, priorities & constraints across various job scheduling scenarios of increasing complexity.
  - Data provided as CSVs drives the scheduling model, using dataframe fields for tasks, resources & materials.
  - Future direction: predictive maintenance, potentially with explainable AI.
