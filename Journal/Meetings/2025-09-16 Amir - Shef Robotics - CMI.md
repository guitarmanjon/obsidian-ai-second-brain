## Key Discussion Points

- EPSRC call under discussion:
  - Event-driven cameras that only record changes to an environment; a neuromorphic world model.
  - Robotics angle - the "slippage" challenge: instead of increasing grip force, the trajectory is changed. A predictive controller loops through possible actions for the robot to find the one that minimises slippage (e.g. tilting your hand when holding a wet phone with wet fingers).
  - Combining this with a neuromorphic world model to improve predictions - "neuromorphic perception."
  - Application areas: self-driving cars, manufacturing, medical robotics.
