## Attendees

- [[JS]], [[HB]]
- [[Hesti]], [[Richard]], [[Bei]] } [[James Law]]

## Purpose

- To explore and scope potential collaboration between AMRC and COM, including existing opportunities, blockers, and next steps.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Bei]] covered applications of AI and human-in-the-loop, and positioned themselves as the COM <-> AMRC liaison.
- Hot desking in COM raised as an option for AMRC & CM staff.
- Undergrad projects due to be aligned in EEE; there is an MSc in AI.
- 4th year and group projects run under the name "Turing" - described as strong Masters projects that often lead on to PhDs.
- On bids: AMRC asked not to receive 11th-hour requests; AMRC can act as a conduit to industrial partners, but COM needs to engage AMRC early.
- [[Bei]] raised the question of how to find the right people for collaboration.
- REF / Publications: little incentive for AMRC staff to contribute, since AMRC (unlike other faculties) doesn't get additional money for this.
- Blockers to collaboration are fundamentally about finances.
- Collaboration ideas discussed: seminars, interest groups, a mentored RSE <-> IMG link, AMRC Communities of Practice, Research Carpentry sessions (practical sessions e.g. writing papers, access to HPC, open to AMRC staff), and a liaison group.
- A tour took place 14:00-15:30.
- Discussion on where to start with collaboration: an AMRC person attending Research Carpentry at COM, and setting up a liaison committee (proposed for **26th Feb or 26th Mar**).
- Existing opportunities to develop: COMPASS, Cyber work, Tekton (Steve Marr already involved but facing capacity challenges), MISSY ([[JS]] to talk to [[Jamie McGourlay]] at R-R), IBM + SMEs, and MSc/Ugrad "pet" projects.
- COM Research Carpentry confirmed for 26th Feb (Tuesday), with sessions: Cyber - Grace, Tekton - Steve Marr, Data + AI - Jon S.
- Other topic noted: NLP for MRO, Standards, and Welding Standards.

## Decisions

- COM Research Carpentry session set for **26th Feb, Tuesday**, with [[Grace]] covering Cyber, [[Steve Marr]] covering Tekton, and [[JS]] covering Data + AI.
- A liaison committee is to be set up, tentatively **26th Feb or 26th Mar**.
