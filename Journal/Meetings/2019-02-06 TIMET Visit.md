## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

*(Note: the top of this page interleaves two columns - a main "Opportunities" list and a side "Collaboration opportunities" list. Reconstructed below on a best-effort basis.)*

- Opportunities:
	- Robotics in the melting shop (foundry?): manipulating raw material, intermediate machining to remove crust and crown, machining in the lifting ring, welding on the stub.
	- Furnace: current monitoring for door opening; correlate with temperature monitoring already in place (EuroTherm); AFRC doing thermographic analysis/monitoring; feeds into scheduling tools for understanding when parts are loaded/unloaded (vs. current method of applying a half-hour window).
- Side-column notes: collaboration opportunities with furnace providers, Siemens, and other AMRC members (NI?). Timet stated they are open to AMRC capturing sensor data.
- Forge:
	- Die distance actually moved, currently monitored through hydraulic pressure - could this be improved with AI/ML?
	- Linear encoders on sliders, rotary encoders on "pincer" pivots; a trade study at millimetre accuracy could feed into bigger-picture/complete-through-process monitoring.
	- How many times can a billet be reheated? Limits are in place; would be useful to know how often parts are actually reheated, and the impact of reheat (grain coarsening).
	- Work hardening - reheat recovers this.
	- Vibration/audio monitoring - what can be learned about the forge from these signals?
- AGV: swarf bin movement, part movement.
- Automation of cell in test house: small parts/movements/machines; could assist with priority orders; modelling work initially.
- Bulk etch: automation could improve safety (acid involved, unpleasant job, little resistance to automation).
- AI/image recognition: AI analysis of US (ultrasound) images; image recognition for surface defects on etched bars.
- Retrofit of sensors:
	- Vibration monitoring - the big surface milling machine has significant vibration, impacting tool wear.
	- Current clamps - how long are lathes actually running for?
	- "Fingerprint" of a machine via interval checks, every 6-18 months?
