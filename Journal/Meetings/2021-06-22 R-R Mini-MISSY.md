## Attendees

- [[Megan]] ([[Rolls-Royce|R-R]]) - key contact for AMRC in WP3, working on Rotatives KPVs
- [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]]
- [[Harry Burroughes|Harry]]
- [[JS]]

## Purpose

- Align on WP3 scope, data capture platform and data-sharing framework for Mini-[[MISSY]], and surface questions for the wider [[MISSY]] programme.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Megan]] ([[Rolls-Royce|R-R]]) is looking at Rotatives KPVs and the use/value of data - the key AMRC contact in WP3.
- [[Harry Burroughes|Harry]]'s questions: what is the non-value-added process being removed through use of data (business case/ROI)? How does this fit into the overall [[MISSY]] programme?
- [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]] response: MCRL4 is the target maturity level for [[MISSY]]; need to qualify in digital format the input condition, environment behaviour and impact on output; validating the product using a "digital fingerprint" (NGV); moving away from a fixed process regime towards a "process window" regime.
- First part of the data stream will be the [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]] technical drawings - what info they contain regarding required processes/tolerances, and what other data sources exist. Ties into model-based enterprise, standards, and forging data/standards.
- [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]]: no formal closed-loop requirement; material already defined in [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]] material standards; use AMRC/[[AFRC]]'s existing data capture capability for Mini-[[MISSY]] to inform Maxi-[[MISSY]] requirements - [[Megan]] working on KPVs to help define the data required; qualification of forging/machining processes (e.g. NDT) sits under WP1/WP2.
- Question of what the common data-sharing platform should be (cloud, local, etc.) - [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]] fine with whatever is used as long as it's secure; ForumPass not needed to move data around except for final reports/presentations.
- Data analytics methods: [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]] suggested looking at results from the upper and lower limits of a forged component, and considering applying the same approach to machining. Can do ~12 parts with material available at [[AFRC]].
- Machining/forging platform:
  - Hermle C52 a likely contender (Edge could be moved over from NB).
  - [[Dave Curtis|DC]] has concerns about stability, suggested the new DMG Mori NVX platform instead - open questions on what's known about the NVX, whether it needs testing on other work first, and whether an Edge device could be installed on it.
  - [[Megan]] noted that if not Hermle, a gap/risk analysis of transferability to Hermle would be needed, since it's [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]]'s most prolific platform.
  - WFL M30 also an option - [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]] would like the spec of this platform.
- Not directly asked but relevant: what data channels can be captured for each possible platform/KPVs (relates to [[Megan]]'s KPV definition work); what data closes the loop (metrology, hardness, surface finish); concurrent data capture (partially covered elsewhere).
- [[HB]] wants a data capture plan for WP3, covering what's needed from WP1 and WP2.
- Collaboration agreement/IP tracker needed between [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]], AMRC and [[AFRC]].
- Output from WP3: report summarising analytic outputs and data capture - not a long Word doc, could be a PPT storyboard with summary diagrams; data sets to be preserved, structured and accessible; recommendations for future work to de-risk Maxi-[[MISSY]].
- Governance: weekly IPT meetings for everyone; [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]] wants a regular check-in on progress.
- [[JS]]'s own reflections/questions: freedom to explore and suggest; can AMRC propose how data is shared for the full programme based on what's learned (tech review of pros/cons)? [[AFRC]] have their own data-sharing ideas that need exploring. Data analysis methods - exploratory vs. prescribed analysis, to de-risk the future programme; already have a 2-factor approach for forging - could a 2-factor approach be developed for machining too? Seen as an opportunity to explore this space rather than relying on [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]], [[Steve Carter|SC]] and [[DN]] to dictate the approach.

## Decisions

- Use AMRC/[[AFRC]]'s existing data capture capability for Mini-[[MISSY]] rather than building something new, per [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]].
- No formal closed-loop requirement for WP3, per [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]].
- ForumPass not required for general data movement - only for final reports/presentations, per [[Jamie McGourlay|JM]].
