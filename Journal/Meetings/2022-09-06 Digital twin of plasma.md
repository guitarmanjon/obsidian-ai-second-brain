## Purpose

- Adjust the proposal's focus after advice that a pure net-zero framing is not ideal.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Arutian Ehiasarian|Harry]] met with grant writers, who advised that a net-zero focus is not ideal because the energy consumed by PVD itself is not huge; the proposal should instead focus on the need for modernising the PVD industry.
- Net zero can still feature indirectly - e.g. PVD coatings can improve the energy credentials of the glass they're applied to. Improving the reliability and repeatability of the PVD process for this glass is key.

## Decisions

- Proposal focus to shift from net-zero to modernising the PVD industry, with net zero as a secondary benefit.
