## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- [[Dave King]]:
	- Birth simulator project with Limbs and Things - printed electronics onto flexible materials, force sensors, working with PEL, app based around QT.
	- World's largest sintering machine.
- MakeTime.io mentioned.
- Cyber security - GCHQ interested in OT security work with AMRC; University of Sheffield setting up a Centre of Excellence on Cyber Security.
- [[Matt]] (Castings):
	- Lots of data collected on every process, but manually interrogated - equipment not connected.
	- Some processes cannot be monitored due to harsh environments.
	- Paperwork used to track parts as part ID stamps can't always be seen.
	- Barcodes used for setting a part and process.
	- Raspberry Pi widely used for tracking parts, developed by student placements.
- [[TM]]:
	- 2025 manufacturing vision: zero verification, part and process variation accommodated, self-learning CAM, new tech introduction without re-baselining, data-driven decisions and improvement activity.
	- Mahalanobis Distance mentioned as a technique.
- [[Kev Kerrigan]]:
	- Whole-life monitoring of manufacturing process for composite materials (forming, heating, machining, joining, etc.).
	- IIRA v1.8 referenced (uncertain in source).
	- Trigger function built into monitoring proposal and database design - allows data logging to be triggered by particular events for easier later searching.
- [[Chris Freeman]]: general overview of work under his remit.
- [[Aiden Lockwood]]:
	- AI, Informatics and Legacy - lack of understanding in manufacturing of how AI can be used.
	- Current project looking to define this, with demonstrators to industry; likely partner-based project with RR blade casting.
	- IBM GPU capability coming on site in the near future; edge computing through Nvidia systems.
- Next meeting's theme agreed as AI and Informatics.
