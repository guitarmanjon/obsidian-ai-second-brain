- Ethics:
  - Data sharing to cloud platforms - not making data public from factories.
  - Re-use of data - sharing via ORDA for experimental data that is not commercially sensitive; seek permission to share data not generated by the consortium.
  - Work closely with data owners (e.g. Pilkington); obtain consent from data owners for data harvesting activities.
  - Draft ethics text: primary data will come from experimental work at SHU and industrial collaborator sites, cleaned/engineered by the research team, and made available to other researchers via a service such as University of Sheffield's ORDA where not commercially sensitive, with publications referencing the data archive.
  - Sharing of primary data from industrial collaborators will require rigorous review of commercial sensitivity, with permission sought from data providers; shareable data will be anonymised and stripped of commercially sensitive information/identifiers as directed.
  - Secondary source data will be collected from existing curated data archives for model training/validation, checked to ensure any conditions imposed by data authors (e.g. attribution) are followed, or excluded if conditions are too prohibitive.

## Decisions

- Case for support to focus on process efficiency, per [[Arutian Ehiasarian|Harry]]'s updates.
- Data ethics approach agreed: no public factory data, use of ORDA for shareable experimental data, anonymisation of any shareable industrial data, and permission-seeking for reuse of secondary sources.
