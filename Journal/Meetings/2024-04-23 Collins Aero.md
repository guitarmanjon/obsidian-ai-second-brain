## Attendees

- [[JS]], [[NP]], [[Mick Walters]] (AMRC)
- [[Andy Cambridge]], [[Darren Meekings]], [[Scott Paterson]] ([[Collins Aerospace|Collins]])

## Purpose

- Agenda set by [[Andy Cambridge]]: to discuss a strategic vision for technology and systems at [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]], focused on (1) understanding current processes via digital system integration to identify problem statements, (2) industrialisation of key processes - AMRC to spearhead integrating MRL 6 technology into current processes while keeping an eye on future tech not yet available, and (3) a digital systems approach to improving current ways of working (ME functional skillset improvements / digitalisation).

## Key Discussion Points

- Introductions, and reason for the connection from [[Darren Meekings]]: introducing functional partners from [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]] and their roles, and giving an overview of where [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]] are and where AMRC help is needed to drive tech and digital systems.
  - [[Andy Cambridge]] (Automation lead) and [[Scott Paterson]] (Industrialisation lead) are key partners for AMRC, to be kept informed but not actively involved, ensuring central alignment.
  - [[Darren Meekings]] is now the key sign-off for all AMRC-related work.
- [[Scott Paterson]]: Industrialisation lead at the Wolverhampton site; shop floor implementation is key, rather than "science projects".
- [[Andy Cambridge]]: focus on automation and the connected factory.
- [[JS]] raised the issue of whether the right people are involved, e.g. TMG.
  - [[Darren Meekings]] wants to ensure everyone who should be included is on the call, otherwise the work won't proceed.
  - Mike Clements from [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]] has worked with TMG directly in the past, but TMG representation is still needed.
  - Impression that [[Darren Meekings]] wants Heads of Group (HoGs) in the room.
- Proceeded through the pack anyway, covering:
  - Data currently collected with little thought as to why - need to step back and understand the purpose of data collection.
  - Pulling in technology that is past MRL 6/7.
  - Being experts in "our process".
  - Factory simulation to understand shop floor performance.
  - Avoiding analysis paralysis.
  - Pareto analysis (80-20).
  - MRL 6+ technologies.
  - Question of how far ahead [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]] are looking - currently no further than Horizon 2.
  - MBD, [[AI]] etc. - how to upskill MEs (manufacturing engineers) in these areas.
  - Context from [[Darren Meekings]]: change is needed; a "second valley of death" (MRL 6+) is present at [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]] - this is where they most need help from AMRC. Value stream mapping needed to demonstrate that new tech will have an impact.
  - Appetite for change at [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]]: described by [[Darren Meekings]] as massive, but capability to deliver it is lacking.
  - [[Darren Meekings]] is very conscious of asking "why".
  - What next: feels like a workshop is needed to understand the pain points. Value stream mapping is [[Darren Meekings]]'s priority number 1. Suggested an initial call with [[Cansu Kandemir|Cansu]]'s team, and a site visit to better understand the challenges. Proposed a visit to AMRC from the [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]] team, with a return visit from the IMG team the next day. IMG to lead on this.

- [[Darren Meekings]] confirmed as key sign-off for all AMRC-related work with [[Collins Aerospace|Collins]].
- [[Andy Cambridge]] and [[Scott Paterson]] to be kept informed but not actively involved in day-to-day work, to ensure central alignment.
- IMG to lead the follow-up work (workshop, value stream mapping, site visits).
