## Key Discussion Points

- Discussion around acceleration/deceleration of machine tools feeding into simulation - optimisation currently proposes feedrate jumps that aren't physically possible.
- TWS want a more accurate cycle-time prediction, particularly for high-speed machining.
- Discussion around commercialisation strategy - [[Nishaint Saini]] asked whether TWS could license the work or become a commercialisation partner.
- Interest in project work generally.
- Temperature measurement work discussed:
  - UW-Madison friction stir welding process.
  - Dagger drill by Niagara Cutter.
  - Feed optimisation based on temperature.
  - Composite machining - temperature is a good indicator of flank wear (the dominant wear mechanism for composites, unlike some metals where other wear mechanisms dominate).
- Tool deflection work: programmed surface vs actual surface due to tool deflection.
- TWS also interested in sponsoring graduate work (e.g. EngD).
