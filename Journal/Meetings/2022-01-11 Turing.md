## Purpose

- To learn about the [[Alan Turing Institute]]'s Data Study Group (DSG) programme and explore submitting a challenge.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Jules Manser]] has run a data study group with AMRC before, through [[Rikki]].
- A data study group is not a hackathon - a challenge owner poses a problem to a group of PhD-level people, who spend a week or two addressing it collaboratively rather than competitively.
- Preparation is key: a DSG PI (post-doc level) works with the challenge owner to ensure the problem is well-posed.
- Outputs from a DSG: a presentation, and a report (drafted by participants during the DSG, finished by the PI, peer-reviewed at [[Alan Turing Institute|Turing]]) - the report gets published. A secure analysis environment can also be provided.
- A challenge owner handbook exists, explaining what [[Alan Turing Institute|Turing]] is looking for and how to interact with the PI.
- Next steps: look at the Challenge Owner pack, submit a challenge proposal (which goes through iteration), then [[Alan Turing Institute|Turing]] finds a DSG PI to do the prep.
- The next DSG opportunity is in September, requiring 6-7 months of prep time.
- Budget for running the DSG was queried, possibly via [[Rab]].
