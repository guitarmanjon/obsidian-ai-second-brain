## Key Discussion Points

- Understand the aims, objectives, and expected outcomes of the discovery project for [[Razor]]'s support in NextGen, and review the long-term ambitions / "what success looks like" for NextGen and the [[AMRC Data Cloud]] (owner: [[Jon]]).
- NextGen success criteria: 14 companies onboarded with active data capture; benefits of data being realised by companies (even if only [[Fourjaw]] data for now); data champions within companies able to ask better questions of their data; AMRC access to all data via the [[AMRC Data Cloud]] with dashboards showing activity across all companies; AMRC Flightdeck running with visualisations demonstrating NextGen, portable enough for tradeshows as well as living in F2050; three hackathon events completed engaging local data talent (professional and student) with demonstrable insight; media output showing NextGen's success, generating interest in further data work and benefiting local tech companies.
- [[AMRC Data Cloud]] (ADC) success criteria: NextGen data starts populating ADC, with companies comfortable with long-term anonymised retention; media output demonstrating ADC value drives further interest and experimentation; projects increasingly use ADC, growing the data lake for future research; tech companies able to access the lake to develop new solutions.
- Need a long-term timeline covering internal/external factors: Digital Champion training, marketing outputs, funding-focused deadlines, and initial [[Fourjaw]] integration completion (owner: [[Jon]]).
- Funding deadline: 31st March - budget must be spent and the majority of project activities completed by then.
- Top-level timeline: 20th January - cut-off for onboarding companies/EyUp applications; 27th January - absolute EyUp application deadline; 6th February - EyUp course starts (runs 6 weeks); 28th February - first hack event; 10th March - second hack event; 25th March - third hack event/showcase.
- Longer term: April 2023 - second EyUp cohort; January 2024 - [[Fourjaw]] subscriptions funded by NextGen end.
- Need to capture AMRC's understanding of the ideal output for participating companies and the competencies required of digital champions (owner: [[Jon]]).
- Need to understand which elements AMRC wishes to manage/undertake itself (owners: [[Jon]], [[Ryan]]).
- Review needed of AMRC Research's [[Data Science]]/[[Machine Learning|ML]] work applicable to data-intensive manufacturing, with input from data scientists e.g. [[Lindsay]], [[Tim]], [[Rogrigo]] (owner: [[Jon]]).
