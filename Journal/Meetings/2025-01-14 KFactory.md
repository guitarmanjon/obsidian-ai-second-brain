## Key Discussion Points

- [[KFactory]] platform focus: connecting manufacturing and IT, focused on industrial digitalisation. Components: Core (collect the data), Analytics (data transformation), Knowledge, and Engineers.
- 95% of customers use the platform on the cloud - cloud-based SaaS is preferred, though on-prem is possible if cloud isn't an option.
- Company is Romania-based and setting up a UK office in Warwick; already talking to [[WMG]].
- Core proposition: asset connectivity and data transformation into useful information ([[KFactory]] Core); a Business Intelligence platform aimed at getting companies to move away from Excel and paper. Lack of data recognised as the biggest challenge for customers.
- [[Machine Learning|ML]] & [[AI]] positioned as the next step for the platform.
- "Virtual Engineers" concept: unstructured comms (e.g. via WhatsApp) is common in factories; some tasks (e.g. Excel jobs) can be automated. Virtual Engineers assist with operational jobs via a single channel/chat conversational application, streamlining comms; links between CRM, [[ERP]], SAP etc. and users via this interface. Factories and maintenance are the focus.
- General theme: "digital transformation to be more sustainable." [[AI]] in manufacturing described as very early days, though [[KFactory]] already has Virtual Engineers deployed.
- [[KFactory]] is targeting a grant this year - a startup with limited resources, having completed[[Vlad Cazan]]investment rounds last year and targeting another this year. Interested in acting as tech provider for an industrial partner on a grant-funded project.
- Sustainability angle discussed: real-time emissions tracking, potentially based on National Grid data - a "Green Virtual Engineer" concept. [[CW Fletcher]] mentioned as a possible connection.
- [[KFactory]]'s grant preference: not concerned about consortium size, but requires a manufacturing focus and a genuine intent to deliver; prefers smaller, more focused consortia. Possible additional [[AI]]-focused grants expected following recent government announcements.

## Decisions

- Agreed intent to identify a suitable grant and apply together, with [[KFactory]] acting as tech provider and needing an industrial partner.
