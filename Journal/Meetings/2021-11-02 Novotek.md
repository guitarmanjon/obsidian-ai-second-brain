## Attendees

- [[Sam Kirby]] (Novotek)
- [[JS]]

## Purpose

- Meeting with Novotek to explore connectivity solutions and the CPL410 device, including a presentation of AMRC's own work.

## Key Discussion Points

- Novotek's Centre of Excellence in Leeds showcases a model factory with full input/output connectivity, visualised with GE Digital software.
- Novotek are UK reps for GE Digital and Emerson hardware, and are working with AFRC using HighByte (Intelligence Hub from HighByte, GE Operations Hub).
- [[SO]] gave a presentation on the Node-RED flow used (time constraints meant a full script wasn't completed) - discussed whether OPC UA is built into Node-RED (a contribution exists for Node-RED).
- Questions raised: is there a reliable OS on the Emerson device (which OS?), plug-and-play support on future devices, control aspects of the CPL410, and connection to non-OPC-UA controllers (potential issues here, may need C/C++ installation on the CPL via a Linux terminal).
- Opportunities discussed: applications beyond machine tools or custom-built machines where PLC access is properly defined; Softing UA gateway for exposing OPC UA connectivity where none currently exists.
- CPL410 closes the gap between PLC and PC by combining them on one device - potential for future cells to use both PLC and PC capabilities.
- Mentioned [[John Carmichael]] and possible Sellafield work with NAMRC.
- PACEdge 2.0 now available.
