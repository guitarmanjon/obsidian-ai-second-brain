## Purpose

- Understand AMRC's view on where digital solutions drive the biggest benefits, and what maturity level is needed to see organisational impact.
- Explore foundational use cases: condition-based monitoring/forecasting (digital M&R), performance visibility (line performance/downtime), and material visibility (real-time consumption vs bill of materials).

## Key Discussion Points

- Original ask from [[Slavomir Pudis]]: hasn't found a business case for predictive M&R so far; interested in condition-based monitoring with forecasting, performance visibility, and material visibility use cases.
- Introductions: [[Bobby Manesh]] (Lead Research Engineer, AI, AMRC Cymru); [[JS]]; [[Slavomir Pudis]] (Productivity Lead, MEU confectionary, 21 sites).
- [[Slavomir Pudis]] background: 12 months ago began a productivity method review, moving away from a purely cost-cutting exercise towards a multi-year lean sigma programme; automation a big driver, payback ideally within 2 years.
- Cobots explored - some applications found but also limitations.
- AGVs also explored; ERP integration has been a challenge.
- Digitisation is the 3rd pillar of "Factory 2025" - still at the beginning of the journey; [[Slavomir Pudis]] is selling the vision internally (big data, etc).
- Digital adoption toolkit - how to implement the change management.
- Augmenting lean manufacturing with digitisation; understanding losses needs to be connected to digitalisation.
- People involvement - opportunity for the 50 affected sites to feed back on proposals.
