## Purpose

- PhD project overviews from COM (Computer Science) and a discussion on how to progress testing research collaborations between COM and IMG, including links to the MADE4Man project.

## Key Discussion Points

- PhD overviews:
  - [[Neil Walkinshaw|Neil]] - supervising student [[Harry]]. Question: how much effort is needed to test a software system? Focus on "fuzzing" - very energy-consuming; testing to a point where things are "good enough." Looking at this in the context of AMRC's cyber-physical space and networks of sensor-based systems.
  - [[Jose]] - using causal inference for experimentation and optimisation of manufacturing processes and existing digital twins. [[Jose]]'s background is in automated software testing; [[Neil Walkinshaw|Neil]] has a background in causal inference. Will need input from AMRC for manufacturing context. Linked to MADE4Man, where [[Pete C]] is involved (access to machines, data, etc.); [[Stephen Marr|Stephen M]] to take over as AMRC supervisor.
  - [[Phil McMinn|Phil]] - robotic skills for manufacturing: simulating robots vs real life, and transfer of robot skills between different robots. Search-based testing - specify the failing point and use heuristic methods to search for behaviours that cause it ([[Basile Marquier|Basile]] co-supervising). Described as a "battleships" approach to testing - faulty behaviour is unknown, so you take shots at the search space to find faults by optimising your shots.
  - [[Donghwan]] - undergrad projects, collaborating with [[Shuwen Wen|Shuwen]] and [[Matthew]] (Brown?). X-ray testing - automating analysis using [[AI]] systems.
- Discussion on how to progress testing research collaboration between COM and IMG:
  - Should there be a testing strategy in IMG? [[Stephen Hallam-Bowles|SHB]] noted there had been a testing strategy previously but it needs updating to encompass current interests in the room, particularly software testing.
  - [[Neil Walkinshaw|Neil]] is keen to align research with real-world AMRC work; a strategy would help guide the research.
  - [[SM]] felt a formal strategy wasn't the priority - better to establish best practice first, as testing isn't yet an area of strength.

## Decisions

- [[SM]] - decided not to pursue a formal testing strategy at this stage; priority is to establish best practice first.
- [[Stephen Marr|Stephen M]] to take over as AMRC supervisor for [[Jose]]'s MADE4Man-linked project.
