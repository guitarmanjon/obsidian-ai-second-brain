## Purpose

- Mini-[[MISSY]] meeting to discuss the uplift/Net Zero element of the project and agree how AMRC will approach sustainability measurement.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Jamie McGourlay|JMc]] presented thoughts per the SoR, matching what [[Donka]] described in the last meeting.
- [[Evalina]] and [[Hamza]] ([[Rolls-Royce|R-R]] interns working on Net Zero with [[Donka]]) will be brought into the project.
- [[Andrew Hamilton|AH]] raised scope 3 emissions - benchmarking against a framework where AMRC's scope 3 would correspond to [[AFRC]]'s scope 1 and 2.
- [[Andy Bell]] confirmed AMRC has tools for the machining aspect and no issue using the Carbon Trust tools/framework.
- Plan to formalise energy consumption per component (carbon output), relying on upstream energy consumption data supplied by [[AFRC]] to AMRC.
- AMRC machining energy consumption to include power draw from the machine tool, coolant pumps, etc. - question of what fully makes up total energy consumption and how to break it down.
- [[Andy Bell]] queried where the boundaries of measurement sit (e.g. systems around machining like air pumps, coolant) - much more complicated than it first appears.
- [[Jamie McGourlay|JMc]] - building understanding of this topic is the key output of the project.
- [[Andy Bell]] asked for [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]]'s definition of "sustainability"; [[Jamie McGourlay|JMc]] said it's used interchangeably with [[Rolls-Royce|R-R]] Net Zero.
- [[DN]] raised waste, energy, and CO2 from the project - tooling, coolant, swarf, recyclability of materials, and minimising cut-ups using sensor data.
- Plan to explore all influences on emissions, material usage, waste, and recyclability for forging and machining, without necessarily putting numbers against everything yet.
- [[DN]] - identify standard methods for assessing Net Zero.
- Proposed a matrix/list of influences on machining's total footprint: facility energy usage; machining energy usage (air lines, hydraulic/coolant pumps); waste materials and recyclability (coolant, tooling, swarf, cut-ups).
- Next steps proposed: capture this data, propose ways to reduce footprint/improve, align with Carbon Trust Scope 2 and 3 definitions, consider [[Andy Bell]] working with Small World Consulting, and reference Jack's calculator.
