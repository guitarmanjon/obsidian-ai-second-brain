## Purpose

- To attend a lecture by [[David Harra]] on the vision for intelligent hole generation/drilling technology and its connectivity roadmap.

## Key Discussion Points

- JS arrived late due to a room change.
- Vision for intelligent hole generation: 180,000,000 holes drilled in 2018.
  - 2018: stand-alone capability - adaptive control, coolant control, cutter break detection.
  - 2019: Oct connectivity added for dashboards and analytics.
  - 2020: geolocation of holes.
- All tools now being ordered are electric with embedded sensing capability.
- Challenges:
  - Connectivity, due to legacy equipment (correct electrical voltage, networking).
  - Human factor - bringing people along on the journey; skills gap in both manufacturing and ICT.
- Getting a "Digi man" project going - identifying trends and what will attract people to it.
- Q: what data is missing?
  - Increase sample rate - currently 20-100Hz, 1kHz desirable.
  - Reduce metrology required - non-intrusive measurement systems.
  - Fastening process - torque data?
