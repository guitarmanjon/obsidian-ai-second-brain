## Purpose

- Explore a potential spinout offering a low-cost monitoring solution for SMEs.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Peyman Moghadam]]'s research group has extensive data experience - visualisation, high-throughput computation with output visualisations and analysis methods.
- Their journey towards Industry 4.0 started via a chat with [[Siemens]].
- Proposed a low-cost monitoring solution for SMEs based around Track, Predict, Optimise, delivered as a no-code platform on a subscription model.
- Currently using vibration and thermal sensors only.
- Working with [[Siemens]] - contact is [[Dave Moss]].
- Existing users (British Steel, Tinsley Bridge, [[Siemens]]) are not SMEs, raising a question about who the solution is really being tested with.
- Testing so far includes a tablet manufacturing demo plant in Diamond, and possibly 8 power sensors.
- No PLC data capture yet - eventual plan is soft sensor development.
- Open questions raised: how/where is it hosted, how accessible is it to end users, and who has it actually been shown to (SME, end-user, etc.)?
