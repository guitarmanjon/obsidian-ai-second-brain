## Purpose

- To explore a potential DigiLab/uncertainty-quantification collaboration for the R-R SMR programme's Module 1.

## Key Discussion Points

- DigiLab has been brought on to do work with Module 1, with a demo shown in Meeting 1.
- Product design is a focus area for this team, spanning both tech and simulation.
- DigiLab overview: their core offering is uncertainty quantification (UQ) using AI, with a heavy focus on UQ generally.
- The PO synced up with DigiLab on Monday, so the team doesn't yet have much hands-on experience/testing with the tool.
- DigiLab is already working with UKAEA, and [[Tim Dodwell]] from Exeter University is involved.
  - > Margin note: Product vs. Service solutions — SMR want a bit of both.
- R-R SMR + DigiLab: DigiLab has experience in the nuclear sector. Use cases discussed include UQ in verification and validation, and the Module 1 demo as a proof of concept.
  - Experimenting with the tool for the SMR team produced 3 demos with tutorials: 1-in-1-out, multi-in-1-out, and multi-in-multi-out (with noise etc.).
  - Also discussed as a capability for the Community of Practice.
- Turning Module 1 back on: potentially straightforward from a digital/DAQ systems perspective, but tricky due to contracting.
- [[Steve Marr]] gave an overview of Module 1 digitalisation.
- On what happens after the POCs with DigiLab: it depends on learning from the exploration phase — lots still to be explored.
- Discussed why not Siemens or another big organisation instead: Siemens don't have as strong an incentive for the work to succeed, whereas DigiLab (a smaller company) has funding that depends on success.
- Other potential data sets raised: robot arm, MaPMoS.
- Question raised about licenses for AMRC colleagues to access DigiLab.

## Decisions

- None formally recorded — this was an exploratory/scoping discussion around DigiLab and Module 1.
