## Purpose

- Continued discussion between AMRC and [[Siemens]] on making machining processes more sustainable, following on from the earlier [[CAM]] sustainability meeting.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Chris Taylor]]'s Catapult work on making machining processes more sustainable.
- Control of auxiliary systems' turn on/off via the machine tool controller.
- Energy recovery from spindle braking.
- Power monitoring using calibrated systems.
- Idle power draw is the same regardless of spindle power, meaning a higher Material Removal Rate (MRR) is more energy efficient.
- Open question on whether servo power consumption data, etc., is available through SINUMERIK Edge.
