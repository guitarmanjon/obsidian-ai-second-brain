## Purpose

- To explore a potential collaboration with [[Siemens]] on making [[CAM]]/machining processes more sustainable.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[RM]]'s cluster at [[Siemens]] is focused on developing technology for more sustainable motion control.
- Four tech focus areas: design for sustainability, design for disassembly, sustainable business models, and sustainable portfolio (changing product offerings to capture new market opportunities).
- Project idea raised: is there a way to make [[CAM]] processes more environmentally friendly and reduce their footprint?
- Low-hanging fruit identified as auxiliary systems on large [[CNC]] machines that are left turned on but rarely used.
- [[Chris Taylor]] is looking at something similar - comparing data captured from power sensors against data from machine controllers, as part of a 2022/23 Catapult project.
