- The 3rd machine has vibration issues, believed to be due to an inconsistent concrete floor.
- Vibration occurs on the 3rd machine but not on the others.
- Core samples taken from the floor found a significant layer of insulation under the concrete.
- Two angles being considered:
  - Is the floor to spec? It was laid near a roller door in winter, with no roller door on at the time.
  - Is it the machine tool itself?
- Considering a tap test on all 3 machines.
- Direct drive motor - had to be retuned?
- Vibration occurs during the turning operation specifically, worst case with a large diameter, tall housing.
