## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Agenda: improving rotor 2 blade cycle times; machining VIGV and rotor 1 panels; VIGV machining; repair tech; AOB.
- AMRC presentation:
  - R2B: [[TMcC]]'s (TM) examples suggested new machines to RR Inchinan. [[Andy Collis]] (AC) asked if this was a blank slate or building on existing capability - RR have 4 existing Mazak 730 machines (different controllers), looking to expand with 3 more (only 1 signed up so far, options on the other 2). Machines are being upgraded (software) to allow data capture.
  - VIGV: vacuum fixtures - prior experience exists but couldn't be shared in this forum. Fixture design work undertaken by [[TMcC]]'s team.
  - NV plugins for flexibility: in-house AMRC tools, not commercialised. [[Dom Wells]] more interested in plugins for feed-rate scheduling etc. (e.g. MachPro).
  - Point cloud to CAD: [[TMcC]] gave an overview of an automated software process (currently VB-based, could be translated to Python, which is approved by RR).
  - [[JS]] presented slides on data capture - some interest shown; [[David John]] would like to follow up.
  - Robotic machining: [[TMcC]] gave an overview of Coroma (robotic-assisted machining).
  - [[TMcC]] gave an overview of what the Machining Group does.
- Follow-on discussion:
  - [[Dom Wells]] raised vacuum fixturing (no further detail captured).
  - General discussion on approach and value added by AMRC; [[PK]] noted the need for requirements from RR. Panels look like a good opportunity to work together - potential in-house programming in NX at RR to build expertise; RLC Calendar makes ~30,000/year, potential outsourcing opportunity; if RR Bristol want to do it themselves, they'd need to demonstrate capability (900/year within 12 months). RR team to regroup and consider how to work with AMRC.
  - VIGV fixturing also raised as an option.
  - Feedrate optimisation tools mentioned: EndPro, ThirdWave.
