## Attendees

- [[JS]]
- [[Iain Crosley]]

## Purpose

- Catch-up between AMRC and [[XpertRule]] (XR) to share updates and explore potential use of the XpertFactory tool at AMRC.

## Key Discussion Points

- XR work since last spoke:
  - Work with [[AFRC]].
  - Microbial growth in the supply chain - sensors for detecting microbial growth in laundry products, led by Procter and Gamble, with the University of York developing the sensors.
  - Applications in bottling, distilling, and the energy sector.
  - OEMs are XR's main customers at present; [[Iain Crosley]] had expected SMEs to be the main customers, but SMEs struggle to define the problems and challenges.
- [[JS]] gave an overview of AMRC work: data security, NextGen (company engagement challenge), and the [[AMRC Data Cloud]].
- Discussion of XpertFactory ("XF Understand") use at AMRC: how to find use cases, and who is the right person to train (a Data Scientist?).
  - Roger Singleton has used it extensively in RISCON (a water monitoring company), in a process engineering context.
- [[MISSY]] project: XR have done work modelling a "golden part/batch". Lack of data is a challenge here - addressed via augmentation of data using mechanistic models, with input/output error used to determine the usefulness of the augmented data.
