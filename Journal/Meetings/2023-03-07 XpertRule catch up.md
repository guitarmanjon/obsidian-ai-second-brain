  - Microbial growth in the supply chain - sensors for detecting microbial growth in laundry products, led by Procter and Gamble, with the University of York developing the sensors.

  - OEMs are XR's main customers at present; [[Iain Crosley]] had expected SMEs to be the main customers, but SMEs struggle to define the problems and challenges.

- MISSY project: XR have done work modelling a "golden part/batch". Lack of data is a challenge here - addressed via augmentation of data using mechanistic models, with input/output error used to determine the usefulness of the augmented data.
