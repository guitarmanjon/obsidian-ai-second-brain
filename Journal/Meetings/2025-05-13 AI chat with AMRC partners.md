## Purpose

- To gauge AMRC partners' awareness of, and appetite for, AI work, and to discuss data ownership.

## Key Discussion Points

- Wanted to learn how much partners know about AI work within AMRC (examples: AI for simulation, tool wear diagnostics, process errors/faults, computer vision).
- Wanted to gauge appetite in partner companies for AI - supporting business processes, supporting manufacturing activities, chat bots, etc.
- Wanted to identify which tech partners people are already working with, if they're able to say.
- Wanted to understand what activities partners want to see from AMRC - demonstrations of applications, LLMs.
- Wanted to know if there are any key tech partnerships partners want to see.
- Data ownership: discussed creating a data repository with research data, made available to the wider University of Sheffield and beyond if possible; also an SDE (secure data environment) for COMPASS.
