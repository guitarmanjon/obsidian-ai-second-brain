## Purpose

- To discuss the proposed approach for the melt shop machining cell project and explore other [[Timet]] collaboration ideas.

## Key Discussion Points

- Melt shop machining cell: [[Harry Burroughes|Harry]] proposed a 3 (really 4) work package approach:
  - WP0 - baseline: 2-3 weeks to understand requirement, basic DES.
  - WP1 - requirements capture and down-select: 3 months.
  - WP2 - DES: 3-4 weeks.
  - WP3 - cutting trials on machines and robot; suggested looking at previous AMRC ADG work for guidance.
- Call with [[Matthew Thomas|Matt]] at [[Timet]]: machining is limited on lathes, taking a long time to centre and set up for a very limited amount of cutting time - raised as a possible limiting factor and driver for new/alternate machines. Discussed a mobile machining platform that moves from melter to melter. [[Matthew Thomas|Matt]] is interested in how research is taken to deployment - AMRC recommendations for how things move to production, suppliers, and collaborators.
- Other project ideas: still in the running but lower priority, still useful.
- [[Harry Burroughes|Harry]] suggested a day at AMRC for [[Timet]] engineers to see current work, develop SoWs, and show work specific to [[Timet]]'s interests.
