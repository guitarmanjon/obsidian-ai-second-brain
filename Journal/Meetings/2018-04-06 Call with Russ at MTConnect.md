## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Research interest in the US around publishing real implementations of factory architecture for new IT systems - opportunity for AMRC to publish on how to implement IT for manufacturing.
- University of Nottingham (seen at MTC) has published machining/manufacturing data analysis work in computer science journals.
- OEM pushback to use their own software (e.g. Siemens) varies - depends who you ask; Euro model (like Apple: one solution for all levels) vs. US model (wanting to do things themselves).
- MTConnect in the UK: Brother CNC noted it's virtually impossible to sell a machine tool in the US without it. UK likely to follow, as UK offerings usually track US/Japan/Germany/China trends. UK is trapped between clear customer needs and being a small market. At least 5 years of development needed. Ongoing agent development, different comms protocols (MQTT), reducing latency.
- Leverage on OEMs/machine tool suppliers: use AMRC's research profile in the digital area to persuade OEMs/vendors to work more closely with AMRC.
- Standard development through TAG (technical advisory group) - a voluntary group - could be used to find contacts for technical support around MTConnect development.
