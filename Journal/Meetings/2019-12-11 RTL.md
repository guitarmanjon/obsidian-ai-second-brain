## Key Discussion Points

The source text on this page interleaves two columns: live meeting notes and the text of an email [[JS]] sent to [[Geoff Berry]] on 30 October 2019. Reconstructed below.

**Meeting notes**
- Discussion around what next (see email context below).
- RTL are starting to use cloud tech for their software - [[Azure]].
- RTL working on equipment usage applications.
  - E.g. HSS hire - container at Heathrow, RTL tech informs on when an asset is taken out of a container; hire period/costs based on this data.
- Goods-in shelving - identifying when goods are on shelves or taken away.
- Frequency of update: visitors might need higher frequency (every 1-2 seconds), assets/goods could be longer (every 30 seconds).
  - Linked to battery life (and hence tag life).
- H&S angle discussed - e.g. slinging equipment that has been quarantined; can tell if it's still being used based on movement, with an alert triggered.
- Fire alarm trigger to list visitors in the building - rapid identification of who is visiting.

**Email context (JS to Geoff Berry, sent 30 Oct 2019)**
- Following on from an earlier meeting, understanding is that AMRC will proceed with a 50-50 split of new tags for people and assets.
- Stories of people struggling to find expensive assets in Factory of the Future make the asset-tagging option attractive.
- Proposed use cases:
  - Tagging expensive assets/frequently required tools that occasionally go missing.
  - First aider badges - rapid identification of first aider provisioning in a building.
  - Visitor tags - using data from large visitor groups to see where most time was spent on the shop floor as an indicator of interest; possibly allowing visitors to wander semi-supervised to see what catches their attention.
- Question posed to RTL: would these use cases also be useful for them?
- A future project may look at digital/Industry 4.0 capabilities on show within Factory of the Future, potentially incorporating RTL data and technology.

## Decisions

- Proceed with a 50-50 split of new tags between people-tracking and asset-tracking use cases.
