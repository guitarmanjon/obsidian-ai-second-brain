- [[Marvin]]: classification vs regression task.

- [[Tool Wear]] Consideration in Model 4:
  - What can be achieved by March 31st 2022?

Should be the same for each part made under a given serial number on average; if there is variation, why (seasonal, shift, machine tool)? Potentially a question for the engineering team or stores team (who do OP10 issuing). [[RS]] thinks it gets serialised early.
