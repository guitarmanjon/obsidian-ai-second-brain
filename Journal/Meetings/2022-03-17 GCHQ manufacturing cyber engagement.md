## Purpose

- Introductory discussion on how NCSC can better understand manufacturing - opportunities, threats, and how AMRC can help.

## Key Discussion Points

- Introductions.
- Task is to assist NCSC in understanding manufacturing - opportunities, threats, etc.
- Reference to Cyberforce, and [[Razor]] Secure (rail cybersecurity company based in Basingstoke) as a comparator.
- NCSC have been tasked (possibly by NSF) to look at Industry 4.0 and understand how cyber fits in.
- Proposal to look at different tiers in manufacturing (OEMs, tier 1, tier 2, tier 3 supply chain):
  - What software is used at each tier, commonalities, and automation tools.
  - Categorise sophistication levels at each tier.
  - Aerospace as first port of call; [[Siemens]] flagged as both a vendor and an end user.
  - [[Roger Badge]] has been asked not to approach this from a vendor angle - focus should be on manufacturers/end-users.
- No fixed timescale for the project.
- Potential outputs: a matrix of technologies vs end users, a lexicon, and data capture/analysis.
- Proposed work packages: define the data capture framework (what to record about companies), internal look at AMRC, internal look at [[HVMC]], and talking to OEMs/tier 1s/tier 2s.
- Suggested follow-on: vulnerabilities already known about (SUNRISE).
