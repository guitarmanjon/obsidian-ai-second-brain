## Purpose

- Catch-up with Splunk to discuss Edge Hub capabilities and potential use cases at AMRC, including status of the "Full Monty" initiative.

## Key Discussion Points

- New Splunk Edge devices: onboard sensing hasn't changed; input available as OPC UA and MQTT, plus sensor input; IO-Link supported via an MQTT bridge.
- Status check on "Full Monty" - question of whether the Edge Hub can handle the required data rates.
- [[Joel]] appears to have allocated a hub to AMRC.
- Existing Splunk Edge Hub use cases discussed: a confectionery company on chocolate mixing machines (vibration data), a crystal company looking at precision of crystal cutters, a German car manufacturer on production line performance - typically bolting the Edge Hub to a machine and using its internal sensors; IO-Link is an easy option since it was supported from the start.
- Potential AMRC use cases raised: can Factory+ be deployed on the Edge Hub? Monitoring in FoF for when the roller door is opened and its impact on machining.
