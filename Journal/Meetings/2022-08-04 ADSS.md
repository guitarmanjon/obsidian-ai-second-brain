## Purpose

## Key Discussion Points

- Mars Express used as motivation - the need for on-craft monitoring rather than reporting back to an Earth base, due to time-lag.
- Head-mounted displays intended to be worn 24/7 by crew.
- Sensors embedded across the spacecraft to give crew an indication of what is happening on the craft.
- Discussion of smart sensors and acoustic ("listening to devices") monitoring.
- Scale envisaged: 5,000-10,000 sensors.
- 2035 is the new target date for Mars Express.
- EASA advised ADSS to look at other harsh-environment applications for their technology.
- Wireless, multi-failure-tolerant sensors discussed.
- Potential applications in JET with [[UKAEA]].
