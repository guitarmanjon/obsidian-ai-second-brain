## Purpose

- Introductory meeting between [[Siemens]] and [[Rolls-Royce]] ([[Rolls-Royce|R-R]]) teams to explore [[Siemens]]' BOPEX product (Bill of Process Execution) and its potential links to AMRC work.

## Key Discussion Points

- Charles had visited AMRC earlier and the chessbot stood out as an opportunity (linked to R3M).
- BOPEX is a product for reconfigurable systems - Bill of Process Execution.
- Alastair presented: BOPEX could link to sustainability demonstrator work.
  - Product flow through the factory represented in software, along with the Bill of Materials (BOM).
  - Need to know the capabilities and skills of each machine in the process, then match this in Teamcentre to the process and allocate equipment.
  - [[JS]] asked about the level of detail needed in the BOP to support this - possibly, but not investigated; likely not a 1-to-1 mapping of assembly to disassembly. BOPEX can be used in MRO.
- Auto conveyor system in Hamburg - carriers have auto IDs and are associated to orders when they become available, and hence assigned to a BOP.
- Blockchain mentioned as a possible mechanism for trusted traceability.
- Open question raised about a potential R3M link-up.
