## Attendees

- None recorded - notes are pasted from an email outlining a Konektio partnership proposal.

## Purpose

- Outline objectives and shape for an AMRC-Konektio partnership ahead of Konektio's internal Business Review meeting on 11th-12th July.

## Key Discussion Points

- Retrofit to legacy lathe/mill and a CNC platform (possibly in FoF), initially thought of as a two-to-three year partnership based on donation of the software subscription and demo equipment.
- AMRC could use AssetMinder with its own data if desired.
- Considerations: cellular network vs IT infrastructure; AR and remote worker assistance on the roadmap.
- Partnership objectives: support AMRC's digital innovation to improve manufacturer performance using Konektio's products; be available to help on specific projects; be AMRC's ThingWorx delivery partner; augment AMRC teams through sponsoring development projects (environmental, energy, productivity, availability, quality); develop awareness of Konektio/AMRC via AssetMinder; be exposed to new technologies and trends; joint work on a government-supported R&D project (reviewing government target funded areas).
- Tangible outputs proposed: demonstrators at Factory 2050 (AssetMinder Predict on Bluetooth retrofit sensors, AssetMinder Impact on retrofit meters), AMRC refreshing its retrofit display, customers exposed to Konektio solutions, AMRC positioned with a strong sustainability story, Konektio getting product feedback; academic input into AssetMinder Predict/Impact/Output/Resolve, building predictive AI/ML offering and improved alarm management/UX.
- Konektio's internal Business Review meeting was scheduled for 11th-12th July, with an offer to discuss further with AMRC there.
