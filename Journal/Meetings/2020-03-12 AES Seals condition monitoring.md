## Purpose

- Introductory session on AVT Reliability / AES Seals' condition monitoring technology for rotating machinery.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Oliver Pogmore]] presenting on behalf of AVT Reliability, part of the AES group.
- Cloud-based data capture on asset monitoring systems.
- Industries served: chemical, process, bio, oil & gas, and most industries - not yet rail or nuclear.
- Thermography of electrical panels used to identify faults.
- ADA (their analytics system) gives an indication of root cause for a fault (e.g. bearing inner race) using a rules-based system - FFT, harmonic matching to component properties, e.g. bearing fault frequencies.
- Asset agnostic - works with any rotating machinery.
- ATEX certification queried.
- Tan Delta sensors mentioned.
- Bluetooth data capture via a CAT industrial mobile device.
- MEMS technology used in the accelerometers - 5.5 kHz Fmax.
