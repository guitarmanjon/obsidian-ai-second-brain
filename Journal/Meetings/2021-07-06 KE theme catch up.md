## Purpose

- Understand SB's KE strategy and where the team fits within the new IMG working group structure.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[SB]] presented slides on what KE is.
- Fundamentally little change to day-to-day work, but an opportunity to grow.
- Four areas: Electrification, PM&C, ROS, Mobile robotics.
- IMG working groups: Non-conventional machining (automated assembly theme); Robotics and control (automation theme); Knowledge Exchange (KE theme); Smart Factories (Connectivity and [[AI]] theme); HMI (Digital theme).
- Five pillars to [[SB]]'s KE strategy:
  - Facilities - ensuring equipment/instrumentation etc. exists to take research from academia into F2050, and to industry.
  - Skills - ensuring people are skilled up to do the work they want to do; outreach via student projects, PhD mentorship, summer placements.
  - Visibility - dissemination to academia and industry, more conference attendance, customer-friendly literature.
  - Funding - linking funding bodies to IMG's research roadmap, via funding body specialists.
  - Cross-sector - alignment to chapter 2, close work with all themes through working groups, links to departments outside ACSE/CompSci and even wider TUoS, not just low-TRL work.
- Time split: 40-50% on low TRL, 40-50% on higher TRL, ~10% development time (tbc).
- Questions raised for the team: what's needed from [[SB]]? Development of the team's technical capabilities, anything specific? Any immediate risks or issues?
