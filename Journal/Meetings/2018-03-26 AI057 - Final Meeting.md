## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Pre-meeting deliverables review: AMRC has 2 publications completed (17 January 2018); final report to be completed within the next month.
- Exploitation plan: discussion of AMRC's roadmap in this area; AMRC to put together a paragraph on how this work feeds into advanced machining, to allow BAE to plan exploitation (possible link to Sandvik CoroPlus systems; data dashboarding).
- [[Stefan Hickox]] (PEL) Q12 update: cutting trials at AML; AMRC machine has a Mori condition monitoring card enabling data integration on HMI, but AML doesn't have this (significant install cost); BAE would not link the PEL module to machine tools for HMI data display, preferring central control of machine tools and dashboards on PCs next to machines. Tool has accelerometer (XYZ), temperature (thermocouple), and wear sensor (resistance). Further work: additional sensing (wear, temperature, accelerometer, force/Spike?), presenting data back to the user, and feeding into manufacturing intelligence / big data work.
- MO review: objectives state "health of machining centres" - queried.
- [[Paolo Melgari]] (CPI) presentation: initial printing of electronic sensors - ultimately not used due to rough surface of PCBN/PCD.
- [[Luiz Franca]] (Element Six) update.
- [[Adam Lewis]] (NPL): originally planned to use piezo sensors, but the group moved away from NPL - is this still viable? A few other sensor options were proposed - queried whether these have progressed outside the project.
- [[Stefan Hickox]] (PEL) and [[Austin Cook]] (BAE) provided steering to the project: Ti material suggestions, cutting tool costs, digital manufacturing (intelligent factory, in-process verification), alignment of AE data to cutting position (corners, wrap-around); tool wear/breakage of most interest to BAE.
- Where next: possible follow-on project under the Industrial Strategy Challenge Fund (currently open, focused on digital manufacturing; prevalence of i-tool not as prominent); keep an eye over the summer for funding decisions; consider an expression of interest for the challenge.
- Further work identified: tool holder engineering (electronics placement, cable routing, balancing for milling tool); data analysis (correlation of sensor signals for process insight); further embeddable sensors; improving wireless data transfer rate; possible cutting tool provider joining the consortium; materials; rotating tools.
