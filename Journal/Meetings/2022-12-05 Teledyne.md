## Purpose

- To review Teledyne's current state of play and discuss what a digital roadmap for their manufacturing operations might look like.

## Key Discussion Points

- Current Teledyne state of play: composites for aerospace manufacture, paper-based manufacturing, MES with only basic functionality, lots of spreadsheets. BAE have stated they need digital engagement for the future.
- A roadmap is required for reviewing where Teledyne are today versus where they want to be in the future. They already have a vision, which is a good start.
- Markets: Defence (BAE & F35), Civil, Business.
- [[Jon Eyre]] (JE) noted roadmaps are available for digital twin (soon) and for Team Defence work in this space.
- Teledyne are nervous about making a choice now that sets them in stone for the future.
- [[Brian Hynes]] is of the mind that the roadmap is the first step - identifying the pathway, costs, risks, etc. - which then makes the internal sell more viable.
