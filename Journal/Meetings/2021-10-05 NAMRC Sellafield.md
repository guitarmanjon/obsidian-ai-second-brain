## Purpose

- NAMRC/Sellafield discussion to review the roadmap for deploying Industry 4.0 technology and identify a non-radioactive asset opportunity.

## Key Discussion Points

- Roadmap originally developed in 2019 by [[Craig Hamer]] with Sellafield (SF); the essential aim is to deploy more I4.0 tech.
- Roadmap staged as: Stage 1 - previous work; Stage 2 - current project to look for opportunities; Stage 3 - develop ideas; Stage 4 - non-radioactive asset.
- Discussed the Vitrification Test Rig (VTR), which encases dried radioactive material in glass for permanent storage - challenges with SF identified for the VTR.
- Overall project value is £1M+.
- ABB Avant 300 is the current DCS.
- [[Evan Bolle-Jones]] to share slides with [[JS]].
- Question raised over whether the approach/technology needs to be vendor agnostic.
