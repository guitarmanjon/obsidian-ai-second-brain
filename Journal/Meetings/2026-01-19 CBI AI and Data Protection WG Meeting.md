## Key Discussion Points

- [[Alessandro Colasanti]] (DSIT) presented on the **Software Security Code of Practice**, which is aimed at senior leaders with accountability and applies to any organisation that sells software; it was co-designed with NCSC.
- The Code of Practice is structured around **4 themes** — refer to the published CoP for details.
- The CoP currently only mentions vendors in the context of developers, raising an open question about how researchers and not-for-profit organisations fit in, and whether this depends on whether software is being sold.
- The intro to the CoP sets out who it is relevant for and which sections apply to whom.
