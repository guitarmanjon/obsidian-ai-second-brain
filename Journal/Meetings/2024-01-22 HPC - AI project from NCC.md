## Purpose

- Introductory discussion with NCC on an HPC/[[AI]] project opportunity, including Isambard access and an industrial HPC innovation programme.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Amit Visrolia]] gave an overview of Isambard, the access AMRC will have, and an overview of what the project is trying to achieve.
- Amit is putting together an innovation programme on industrial use of HPC - a 2-year Innovate UK (IUK) project, enabling capabilities for application of HPC and [[AI]] to engineering and manufacturing, with industrial use-case demonstrators.
- [[Rolls-Royce]] [[MISSY]] project could/should feed into this.
- The proforma discussed is included in the Statement of Work (SoW) that [[Fairuz Yooseff]] has shared.
- A workshop with industry is planned for 30th January - discussion on who to invite; Amit already has a list of confirmed attendees and will share it; need to identify who from AMRC can support.
- Ask for AMRC this week: flesh out what Aerospace needs in this sector and get ideas down on paper ahead of the workshop, combining what's heard from industry with AMRC's internal goals. Run an internal exercise first to gather ideas across the organisation - what are the specific industry challenges, which can be answered with [[AI]], and which can be tackled with HPC.

## Decisions

- Agreed that the [[Rolls-Royce]] [[MISSY]] project should feed into this HPC/[[AI]] programme.
