## Key Discussion Points

- Itinerary: 10:00 arrival at F2050 (IMG capabilities with Alex and Jon; NW capabilities with Zohaib); 11:00 meeting with AMRC CTO Ben Morgan; 12:00 lunch, head to DPG; 13:00 DPG capabilities and tour (David King); 13:30 TMG capabilities and tour (Pete Crawforth or delegate); 14:00 CC capabilities and tour (Matt Smith); 14:30 return to F2050 / visit to Castings (TBC); 14:45 Castings capabilities with Jack Naylor; 15:00 AMRC Cymru capabilities with Kevin Chan.
- Purpose of the visit framed around real capabilities, growth areas, and support from HVMC to open doors - these capabilities need sharing internally.
- Rab gave an overview of the AMRC Digital strategy; JS gave an overview of DC&AI; Alex gave detail on Smart Factories.
- Zohaib presented the NW digital mindmap, covering: Industrial Connectivity; Human-machine shared space (with sub-areas AR/VR, Smart building, Knowledge capture); AI for Industry 4.0; Machining informatics.
  - Separate "to look at later" list noted alongside: Black Shark AI (MSFS link, 3D world rendering); Gartner hype curves; Horizon scanning; Mike Hinton - "Customer Friend" white paper.
  - Project presentations: InKA - an NLP search tool across AMRC data sources to avoid repetition of projects, used across 60-70 SME projects (a "local ChatGPT"?); 5G - Chain of Custody Systems, logistics link-up using 5G; Smart workbench - uses AI for recognition of parts and actions, and presence/absence of parts.
- Kevin Chan (AMRC Cymru) - noted a follow-up planned for 09/03/2023. Agritech and food & beverage are big drivers for Cymru's work. Digitising humans was raised, with a need to be careful of the human aspect given the unions present in companies like Airbus. Future opportunities: AI/ML (helping companies understand what it is), Agritech (implementing digital and understanding how it can help), Hydrogen/alt energy/SMR, and digital skills in SMEs (not usually in-house).
