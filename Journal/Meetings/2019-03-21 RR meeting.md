## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Agenda: Samulet 4 ideas, Bleed valve project.
- RR context: S&T (Structures and Transmission) and Compressors now combined.
- What tech is on the horizon that could be built into a project? Digital examples raised: real-time vibration monitoring, adaptive control of processes/parameters.
- [[Sam F]]: roadmap aligned to Paul's (S&T?) components; stresses and vibration is a focus area; HPIP project; working with Omer looking at power draw etc. on the Hermle (prediction vs. real-data); Catapult project looking at creating a dynamic profile from simulation of a part (part, fixture, machine, tool interaction); Sam interested in what H5 has to offer.
- [[Jon S]] gave an overview of the ABG proposal.
- Discussion around adaptive machining: using simulation data in correlation with real-data; [[RF]] asked how the program is adapted if something is drifting.
- [[RF]]: could a part be machined "wrong" such that when released from the fixture the stresses correct it? [[Sam F]] noted order of cutting is usually defined by the drawing itself, so this line of thinking often isn't possible.
- [[Will]] raised probing - end of cycle validation probing, asked what [[Sam F]] does with this. Points raised: releasing clamps needs to account for impact; possibility of inferring end of cycle from in-process measurements; [[RF]] noted this is being looked into.
- [[JS]] raised inspection by exception as an idea (detail unclear).
- [[Paul]]: suggested maybe bringing together supply chain with RR to fund work instead of Samulet 4; noted concessions in the supply chain/RR - around 60 people in S&T working on concessions.
- Plenty of interest in ABG - question of how it feeds into Sam's Catapult project.
