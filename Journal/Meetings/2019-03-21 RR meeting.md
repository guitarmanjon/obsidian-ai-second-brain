## Purpose

- To discuss Samulet 4 ideas and the Bleed valve project, and explore what future tech could be built into a joint [[Rolls-Royce|RR]]/AMRC project.

## Key Discussion Points

- Agenda: Samulet 4 ideas, Bleed valve project.
- [[Rolls-Royce|RR]] context: S&T (Structures and Transmission) and Compressors now combined.
- What tech is on the horizon that could be built into a project? Digital examples raised: real-time vibration monitoring, adaptive control of processes/parameters.
- [[Sam F]]: roadmap aligned to Paul's (S&T?) components; stresses and vibration is a focus area; HPIP project; working with Omer looking at power draw etc. on the Hermle (prediction vs. real-data); Catapult project looking at creating a dynamic profile from simulation of a part (part, fixture, machine, tool interaction); Sam interested in what H5 has to offer.
- [[Jon S]] gave an overview of the ABG proposal.
- Discussion around adaptive machining: using simulation data in correlation with real-data; [[Richard Foster|RF]] asked how the program is adapted if something is drifting.
- [[Richard Foster|RF]]: could a part be machined "wrong" such that when released from the fixture the stresses correct it? [[Sam F]] noted order of cutting is usually defined by the drawing itself, so this line of thinking often isn't possible.
- [[Will Kinghorn|Will]] raised [[Probing|probing]] - end of cycle validation [[Probing|probing]], asked what [[Sam F]] does with this. Points raised: releasing clamps needs to account for impact; possibility of inferring end of cycle from in-process measurements; [[Richard Foster|RF]] noted this is being looked into.
- [[JS]] raised inspection by exception as an idea (detail unclear).
- [[Paul Turner|Paul]]: suggested maybe bringing together supply chain with [[Rolls-Royce|RR]] to fund work instead of Samulet 4; noted concessions in the supply chain/[[Rolls-Royce|RR]] - around 60 people in S&T working on concessions.
- Plenty of interest in ABG - question of how it feeds into Sam's Catapult project.
