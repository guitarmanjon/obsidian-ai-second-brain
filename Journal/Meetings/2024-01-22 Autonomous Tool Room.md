## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- [[Adam Brown]] (AB) gave an overview: £100k TMG / £100k IMG Catapult funding; thoughts on vision; objectives for the day were to develop collective vision statements and generate the Year 1 Statement of Work (SoW).
- Vision statements discussion:
  - [[Rob Ward]] - very data-centric view; RB's point on existing tech is that it must be open comms/API etc.
  - Rodin (a Dutch company) noted as the closest existing industrial company to an autonomous tool room.
  - [[Adam Brown]] - human in the loop, data is critical.
  - [[Phil Yates]] (PY) gave an overview of automation work already done at AMRC, including Sandvik's automated changing of indexable tooling, and raised the interoperability question - why is it so hard to swap out a sensor in an automated system? Wants USB plug & play style connectivity.
  - [[Tom McCready]] (TM) - a user of the system sees cost and time of making a component as feedback.
  - [[Rich Bonnell]] (RB) - even manual interventions can be tricky, e.g. repeatably changing an insert 100% of the time.
  - [[Stephen Marr]] (SM) - favours micro-services over a single behemoth system.
  - [[GH]] - let's not make bad decisions now that limit what can be done later.
- Shared Google Drive folder for the project: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1H9BEZDM-7TsA-SHLOd9s9-0SdmXfnBc2
- Autonomy vs. automation identified as different concepts: autonomous implies a decision-making system (e.g. AI); automation is "very 70s / Industry 3".
- LIGER mentioned - swarm optimisation talking to multiple CAM systems.
- Parallel points/questions raised for the vision: right data in the right place for the right thing at the right time; data availability/safety/anonymity for research use (AMRC Data Cloud, Data Connect, etc.); how many parts are being planned (400 apparently); should not detract from machining expertise; where does human-in-the-loop fit in, and how is knowledge capture handled; what is the boundary of "autonomous"; feedback from production data into the CAM process.
