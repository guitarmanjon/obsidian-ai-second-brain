## Purpose

- Initial call to explore whether AMRC could deliver a 5-day assist to help DataForm Lab (an SME in the construction supply chain) with a Data & AI Strategy, funded by a small (~£2k) budget tied to an EU grant that needs to be spent quickly. [[Cameron Christie]] brokered the introduction via Slack on 22 May, asking [[JS]] to speak with Eva.

## Key Discussion Points

- DataForm Lab has its own in-house software to collate factory capabilities within the construction supply chain (e.g. timber frame manufacture - what sizes/designs can be manufactured) and provide comparisons between factories.
- They also help set up robot cells and do programming based on parametric design.
- DataForm is trying to automate the services they deliver.
- They are collecting a lot of data on manufacturing, production, design, procurement, and quotation data at the customer sites they work with.
- They can currently simulate and evaluate; optimisation is important to them - specifically optimisation algorithms and what can realistically be achieved.
- Technical data on the production equipment is also available to them.
- Reference: https://www.dataformlab.com/
