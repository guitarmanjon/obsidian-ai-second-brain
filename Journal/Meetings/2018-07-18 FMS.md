## Key Discussion Points

- Discussion of latest IFDR results from NVX; Rolls-Royce grinding need for filtration/coolant management; 4 IFDR systems already in place at Derby (Rotatives).
- Oracle (and other) systems don't look at particle size, only concentration, pH, and temperature - particle size has a significant impact on surface finish. RR seeing improvement in smear and pickup after adopting IFDR.
- Innovate UK:
	- Existing tech call, focused on UK adoption.
	- FMS have trouble getting their system into companies because OEMs provide their own coolant filtration systems and are reluctant to allow third-party solutions; RR is on board with coolant cleaning/filtration and adopting IFDR.
	- Route would be straight to trial funding, with pre- and post-analysis needed, and an evaluation plan plus evaluation partner required.
	- [[Steve Kirk|Steve]] already has a cost-benefit partner.
	- AMRC proposed as partner for machine health - capturing data before and after implementation.
	- IFDR resolves to monitor for: pump and valve wear (will wear be seen over the test period? InsightCM referenced); spindle failure (seals etc., energy consumption, torque, coolant contamination vs spindle power); tool life (SMEs unlikely to monitor this, so AMRC to do comparative wear measurements); temperature at cutting tip (flagged as tricky).
