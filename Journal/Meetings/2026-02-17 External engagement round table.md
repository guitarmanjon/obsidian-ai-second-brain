External conference engagement - presented the AMRC portfolio (UNS/practical network-building) at ARC (a large automation conference), and has been invited to speak at the European "Industry 4.0" community Discord server. Now building a UNS community of practice - an informal shared space (ROS, vendors, industrials) to align on what UNS actually means to different groups.
- [[Narcisa Pinzariu]]: previously involved with a Vision community of practice, engagement has dropped off recently, partly through being busy and partly not getting the engagement wanted. The "researcher forum" (run under IMG by [[Steve]]) was valuable for cross-group discussion but has since lapsed. Currently: leading strategy for visual inspection standardisation across Rolls-Royce (~7 people); the AI validation project across HVMC (year 2, now testing the framework built in year 1); some Quantum work, though funding has reduced this year.
- [[Edwin]]: runs "AI Talks" internally, trying to engage people from different groups on AI/ML topics - good engagement generally, though it's been harder recently to find presenters. Helping [[Alex Godbehere]] organise the UNS community of practice, including possibly inviting external speakers (has previously brought in a company via a University of Newcastle contact); works closely with machine vision suppliers.
- [[Jon Hall]]: presented connectivity work in various external forums, including a (now-defunct) PTC ThingWorx community session - presented just before PTC sold ThingWorx - and a working group adjacent to the World Economic Forum. Involved in a large EPSRC weaving project consortium (with Leeds University and suppliers), mostly coordination/communication support to [[Jody]]'s team who lead the technical work. Echoed that the researcher forum was valuable for its technical depth and lack of bureaucracy - worth reviving. Mentioned [[Grace]]'s work building cyber-security community consortia, and a (now-defunct, funding stopped) government-funded networking body that had been useful for university connections.
- [[Tim]]: part of the university's Data Stewards Network (run via the main library, cross-university) - low commitment, monthly meetups plus a Google Chat channel, valuable for making connections (e.g. into research software engineering, useful on the TR project). Also part of a data-sharing working group set up by [[Sarah Hayes]] (Digital Twin Hub) - a senior, cross-sector group influencing government strategy on data sharing; noted that [[Jon Stammers]] is a prominent figure in that group too.
- [[Alex Godbehere]] (continued): involved with CMI (Centre for Machine Intelligence) leadership team, trying to build a better link between CMI and AMRC - more to be done, as there isn't yet much joint activity. Mentioned [[David King]] leads a low-profile CMI interest group that's struggled with engagement from AMRC. Involved with the IET Manufacturing Technical Network committee - informal, deciding webinar topics/speakers (the likely source if anyone gets asked to present for the IET). Less involved now in HVMC's Digital Special Purpose Group - [[Amelia]] is now the main link, as she has the broader view across AMRC that's needed for that role. Also connects with Research Software Engineering at the university via [[Steve Marr]], plus contacts [[Raman]] and a separate [[Edwin]] who leads the RSE team - looking at closer collaboration between RSE and Digital/IMG.
- [[Jon Stammers]] (closing note): [[Chris Greg]] approached JS about a pot of Compass "risk budget" funding for short projects (likely 2-3 months) with relevance to Compass - a lightweight, roughly one-page submission process expected soon. Flagged as a heads-up for anyone with relevant ideas.
