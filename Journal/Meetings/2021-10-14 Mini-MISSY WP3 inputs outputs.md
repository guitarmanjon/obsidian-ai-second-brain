## Attendees

## Purpose

- Mini-MISSY discussion to clarify WP3 inputs and outputs, and data format requirements.

## Key Discussion Points

- AFRC is forging the parts for the project and shared a video capture of the process.
- WP4 is not happening currently - funding is on hold.
- Discussed whether there's clarity on what will be captured during forging and machining, and in what data format.
- [[Martin Rawson]] raised the potential for a change in machining response due to the radial forging method - could depth into turning be compared against other machine tool signals to identify a change? Radial forging mainly deforms the outside of the part.
- Data output considerations: RPM vs m/minute conversions, and timestamp format - agreed to use ISO 8601 for timestamps.

## Decisions

- Use ISO 8601 for the timestamp format.
