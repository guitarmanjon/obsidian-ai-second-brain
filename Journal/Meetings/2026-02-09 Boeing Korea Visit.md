> [!note] What was talked about?

- **Agenda**: Korea E&T capabilities, discussion on overlapping areas, and a tour of F2050.
- **Intros**: [[DL]] mentioned a new Boeing aircraft planned for the mid-2030s, possibly to be announced this year. [[CH]] also introduced themselves.
- **Boeing dept structure**:
  - APA - Advanced Production + Automation.
  - M+S - Materials + Structures.
  - BGS - Boeing Global Services (defence MRO, large UK presence).
- **BKETC AI overview** (presented by [[Soohwan Bae]]):
  - BKETC collaborates with other Korean companies (Hyundai, Samsung, etc.) on tech stack & data for manufacturing.
  - Side discussion on the HR+ model and the tech stack/pillars required for it, on the back of hiring, including lessons learned from Microsoft on HR.
  - AI @ BKETC has 26+ people, organised around four **AI Domains**: Perception, Predictive & Decision making, Generative, and MLOps.
  - Three areas of Boeing work: Engineering Productivity, Products + Services, and Production Systems.
- **Warehouse 4.0 projects**:
  - Mini Warehouse 4.0 project using a **Vision System** for part counting, particularly for small components where weight-based counting is too inaccurate.
  - Inspection Plan Knowledge Extraction, covering supply chain traceability, digital thread, and "Chain of Custody" - challenge is the manual quality checks on parts as they travel up the supply chain, leading to long inspection times due to paperwork.
  - DIPA - Document Intelligence + Parsing Assistant.
  - Warehouse 4.0 also has a vision system element for defect detection, using a Vision Language Model.
  - Parts sales forecasting.
- **Production Systems projects**:
  - Restricted Materials / Dangerous Goods - automating inspection of documents to extract relevant info, and checking correct labelling of redistributed goods.
  - NCR assessment - using AI to compare attached parts against inspection plans / assessment documents.
  - Boeing Predictive Assembly Tech - scanning components to be attached to find optimal shims.
  - Voice-driven NCR Automation - voice-to-text to capture NCR reviews, with structured output; challenge is the very noisy environment, requiring denoising tech.
  - Customer Experience - speech-to-text for hard of hearing flyers, plus translation; domain-specific terminology is a barrier for standard STT systems (Apple, Google, etc.).
- **Productivity projects**:
  - Corrective Action Plan - created when the FAA requires a change; system looks for previous relevant docs and guides users through the process.
  - Data input - addressing manual processes that can lead to missing data, via automation to avoid the gaps.
  - Hyundai Motor Group in Singapore (HMGSIC) - noted as having 3 floors of car.
