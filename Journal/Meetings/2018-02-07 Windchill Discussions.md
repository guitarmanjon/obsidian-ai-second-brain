## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- [[TMc]] gave an overview of what FoF is trying to do, to understand parallels with IMG/F2050. Key components: paperless, NC files.
- [[BM]] gave an overview of IMG's Windchill history: used Windchill 7 years ago but it wasn't configured for how they wanted to use it; moved to K: drive (fine, but no doc sign-off/automation); now working directly with PTC to implement their products, with an audit as motivation. Suggestions made to PTC included doc numbering conventions (01, 02 etc. at end of doc name) and embedding a signature in the document for PDF requirements.
- [[RS]]: work started years ago to capture what each group needed from Windchill; concurrent engineering looking at how the AMRC/Concurrent partnership will progress. [[TJ]] asked where the promised test environment was - [[CV]] has chased regularly with no success. [[RS]] to meet with Concurrent soon.
- [[TJ]]: new doc types become visible to everyone once added - [[TJ]] and Matt Bacon added some but removed them due to this visibility issue. To resolve, policies would need removing from all ~1650 existing projects; a script is needed to do this.
- [[CH]] raised the need for a Business Systems committee. [[CV]] noted there are now 4 or 5 instances of Windchill and questioned why.
- [[TMc]] doesn't want a committee, as it would delay work. [[RS]] countered that it would help drive forward how systems/ideas are shared; delays will lead to research teams going their own way ([[BM]]: this is why IMG are doing the current PTC work; [[RS]]: Medical went off and did their own thing without discussing first).
- [[BM]] keen to get a beta implemented to understand capability and baseline, and to share/demo the system so all can comment on spec and functionality.
- [[CV]]: the multi-instance problem exists elsewhere too (e.g. IMS and similar systems across AMRC, NAMRC, IMG/AIMS).
- [[RS]] departed at 10:39. [[TMc]]: need Colin to determine priorities, i.e. allow for an advert at G8 for a Windchill developer/admin.
- [[CH]]: IT would like to be involved in projects but has never had the resource - always firefighting instead. IT Committee is untenable due to forum size, decisions don't get made - proposed a Business Data/Systems committee instead.
- Test Development environments in Windchill allow check-pointing for easy roll-back. [[RS]] to push Concurrent on giving the test environment. IT is a support service but needs to be more than that.
- [[BM]] will share the spec developed with PTC (possibly via Chris Scraggs); potential to fold AMRC requirements into the IMG spec and use their test environment.
- [[TJ]]: the production environment may not be able to support changes made in test environments.
- [[BM]] and [[TMc]] departed at 10:55. Possibility raised of having to scrap the Windchill element of the tender - needs discussion with Tom.
