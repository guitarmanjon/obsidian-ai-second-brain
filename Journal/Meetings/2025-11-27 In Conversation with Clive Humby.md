## Purpose

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Dr Richard Bruce]] is doing work with [[Andrew Martin]] from Cymru on F&D (food & drink) supply chain.
- Scalability: digital solutions - for example Ocado's robot-based distribution warehouses - can be a hindrance to scaling. During the pandemic, Tesco and Sainsbury's were able to double their home delivery capacity within a couple of weeks by re-purposing in-store staff to pick & pack deliveries. Ocado couldn't do this because its digital system is less flexible than a human-based system.
- Tesco Clubcard data: the shift to more customer-focused stores also led to optimisation of the supply chain, helping supply chains adapt and become more competitive.
- Female Lead: a charity/campaign (https://www.thefemalelead.com/) run by [[Edwina Dunn]], Clive's wife.
