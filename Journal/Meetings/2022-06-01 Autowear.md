## Purpose

- Catch-up with Adam on the status and challenges of the Autowear project.

## Key Discussion Points

- Background: IMG got involved early on but didn't deliver what was really wanted - no integration with the NC. IMG's input to the system has now been removed; the hardware is now pretty robust.
- Opportunity - automatically finding tool edges using focal peaks is doable; next step is integrating this with controller data. [[Javier]] has integrated this with the current TMG solution for data capture.
- Funding is an issue.
- Hardware final review is planned for this year using various digital funding, improving lighting, and line scan vs. area scan.
- Software is lacking - AI is a challenge because many examples of each tool are needed to train the AI system.
- DMG potentially bringing out a commercial solution soon.
- Kennametal ADG funding - lots of wear testing to be done; Kennametal keen on autowear for their own testing purposes, potential to get involved. AI vs. a difference-based approach - variable testing is key, so training AI is possibly not an option.
- Understanding the application is key.
- Estimated 6 weeks of PE (project engineer) time needed.
