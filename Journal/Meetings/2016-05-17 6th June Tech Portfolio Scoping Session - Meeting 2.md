## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Need to prepare a synopsis to send to partners before the session: expand on the title, key talking points for the project, aims of the session, and a brief on funding options.
- Agenda for the 2-hour time-slots:
	- 10 minutes - round-room introductions
	- 20 minutes - setting the scene (introduce synopsis, funding options)
	- 15-20 minutes - brainstorming activity (specific questions per project, consider standard SoW questions, understand whether the project will be one large Innovate-funded piece of work or smaller projects with specific partners/ADG collaboration, capture ideas)
	- 10 minutes - break (AMRC staff bring ideas together into themes/summarise the brainstorming; consider breaking into smaller groups if several themes emerge)
	- 30 minutes - detailed brainstorming activity (further define project ideas, summarise in a project 4-box, statement of requirements)
	- 15 minutes - summarise session with a "finalised" SoR, invite feedback from all
	- Define actions to take the work forward
