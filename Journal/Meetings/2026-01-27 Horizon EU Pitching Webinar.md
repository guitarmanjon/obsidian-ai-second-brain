**Embedded slide: topic overview (HORIZON-CL4-2026-02-DIGITALEMERGING-51)**
- Targets progression from TRL 4-5 to TRL 6. Overall idea: develop innovative, explainable AI-based solutions to enhance competitiveness and productivity in manufacturing by enabling intelligent real-time monitoring and dynamic decision adaptation, with an explicit human-in-the-loop approach.
- Key challenges to be addressed span: limited real-time monitoring and adaptive control; fragmented systems and data silos; inefficient use of materials, energy, and resources; high scrap, rework, and quality losses; poor integration of organisational knowledge; and limited support for human-in-the-loop decision-making.
- Contributions and solutions sought include: real-time production data access; automated diagnostics; AI-enabled predictive quality; proactive decision support to maximise component and material reuse; an adaptive AI-driven prescriptive system positioned as a trusted, safe, secure, always-on assistant for optimal decisions (**LIRIS contribution**); explainable, human-in-the-loop AI for interactive and transparent optimisation; joint efficiency, flexibility and sustainability improvements; support for agile production under variability and uncertainty; and strong alignment with Clean Industrial Deal objectives (energy efficiency, waste reduction).

**Embedded slide: European AI Ecosystem diagram**
- Shown as a set of hexagons covering: TEFs (highlighted/outlined), AI Factories/HPC, EDIHs, the AI Office, the AI Board of Member States, Regulatory Sandboxes, the AI-on-Demand Platform, Dataspaces, EDICs, and the AI Skills Academy — framing the wider European AI support landscape that proposals are expected to connect with.
