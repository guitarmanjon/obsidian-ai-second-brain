## Purpose

- Identify overlaps between Physics department research capability and AMRC industry challenges, and understand available KE funding routes.

## Key Discussion Points

- Trying to work out how to do more KE with the Physics department.
- [[Karim Sorefan]] outlined relevant research:
  - STFC funding doing data science on big data sets (space weather, CERN, etc.) - triggering events.
  - Astrophysics - [[Machine Learning|ML]] for identifying objects of interest, classical maths techniques for sorting galaxies - also STFC funded.
  - EPSRC KE funding - Karim tries to encourage academics to work with AMRC. Academics primarily need help finding partners or understanding industry challenges.
  - Relevant skills available: [[Machine Learning|ML]], data analysis, imaging.
  - Funding is fairly easy to get and good for de-risking projects; TRL doesn't matter much (can be as low as 1-3, though higher is sought after) - return on investment is considered in applications.
- If AMRC has a project, approach Karim, who will look into available funding.
- 6 months is the longest typical duration - usually the concept should be proven by then.
- Cash contribution from partner is usually 10%, up to 50%, with some IP considerations; in-kind contributions also welcome.
