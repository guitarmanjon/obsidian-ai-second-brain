## Attendees

## Purpose

- Catch-up on the EyUp course, reviewing attendee feedback, apprentice engagement, and planning for the next cohort.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Neil Bizzell|Neil]] gave feedback on course attendees:
  - One attendee thought it was a Fourjaw (FJ) training course.
  - Another assumed it was FourJaw training by "putting 2 and 2 together to make 5".
  - One struggler had disorganisation/rescheduling issues and a lack of attachment to the course schedule, but is still on the course.
  - Others were not convinced they knew what to expect from the course.
  - One dropout gave no feedback on why, other than finding it too hard.
  - Overall, the cohort seems happy.
- [[Neil Bizzell|Neil]] is concerned that the attendees are apprentices, who may not have access to some data from their organisation - could AMRC get a FourJaw (FJ) data dump for each company?
- Discussed the idea of running the course over 10 weeks at 2 days per week.
- [[Neil Bizzell|Neil]] raised post-course impact concerns: will apprentices have enough sway in the business to implement change after the course? Need to target more senior employees, or ensure the Ops Manager (for example) is invested in the apprentice doing the course. A "sponsor" should be identified at the application stage, with a follow-up a few weeks after the course to see how things are being implemented.
- Timing of the next course: a 10-week duration means at least one person is likely to have annual leave during it; 2 days per week should be the minimum cadence, otherwise learning is lost in the gaps.

## Decisions

- [[Karen Payne|Karen]] and [[Mai]] to start talking to interested businesses to get a sense of duration and timing preferences, rather than directly onboarding them.
