## Purpose

- First meeting with [[Chris Dungey]], newly appointed National [[AI]] Champion for Advanced Manufacturing, to align on data-space/[[AI]] priorities.

## Key Discussion Points

- Ahead of the meeting, prepared briefing points on [[Chris Dungey]]'s [[AI]] Champion mandate, the digital front door, and a data spaces angle (research assisted by Gemini), plus topics to raise such as skills for data and [[AI]] ([[Lindsay Lee]]), a data-first approach to [[AI]], and human-centred [[AI]] work ([[Sharan]] at Cymru).
- Overview of [[Chris Dungey]]'s role as [[AI]] Champion, tied to the Modern Industrial Strategy and Advanced Manufacturing Sector Plan, which drove the creation of sector champions.
- **Focus on OT for boosting competitiveness** - covering compute, skills, etc.
- Industrial front door is **(Phase 1)** - a webpage currently under construction, including an [[AI]] for Manufacturing definition, a link to MADE UK, and 5 site areas: Understand [[AI]], Explore Applications, Identify Skills, Regulatory Guidance, and Readiness + Support. A QR code is planned to capture top challenges, possibly at MACH.
- Phase 2 is a UK [[AI]] for Manufacturing Strategy and implementation plan - [[Chris Dungey]] has a paper in draft and will share it. Covers 5 areas: Compute + Architecture; Data (talking to [[Jon Gyre]], including digital and data readiness); Skills ([[Lindsay Lee]]); [[AI]] Applications; and Regulation to support innovation.
- An [[AI]] Experience Network is planned across [[HVMC]] - [[MTC]] is spinning one up, and all centres have their own experience to contribute.
- **"Systems V" approach - Systems of Systems**.
- Centres to host outreach activities over the next 6-12 months - conferences, lunch + learn sessions, C-Suite focused sessions.
- MACH plan discussed: [[Chris Dungey]] to give a 10-minute intro covering [[HVMC]] and the [[AI]] champion role, with the author giving an overview of [[AI]] use cases across the network.
