## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Senseye is really targeted at facilities with many assets that are running continuously; accepted that it's not the ideal tool for somewhere like the AMRC
- It's an app in Insights Hub, so data can be shared with other IH apps
- Ideally Senseye feeds alerts to a Maintenance Management System, and also integrates info on work events from the MMS
- Can log savings in time, cost, etc., which feed into overall benefits of using Senseye, such as avoided downtime and savings on parts - KPIs for senior managers
- Compatible with other out-of-the-box data capture methods, such as OSI PI and Thingworx, as well as Siemens Solutions
- Fully documented APIs available so data in/out is doable, allowing data to be utilised in other data analysis platforms
