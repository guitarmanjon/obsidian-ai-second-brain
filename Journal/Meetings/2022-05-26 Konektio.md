## Purpose

- Introductory session with [[Konektio]] to learn about their AssetMinder platform and explore potential collaboration with AMRC.

## Key Discussion Points

- Intro notes: Sensorworks for sensors; AssetMinder is the key technology/product; heavy use of Thingworx.
- [[Konektio]] provide end-to-end edge-to-cloud solutions and an embedded solution for OEMs (e.g. Stannah stairlifts) - servitisation.
- Democratisation of access to these technologies is a key driver for [[Konektio]].
- AssetMinder overview (slide deck):
  - Predict - condition monitoring (most mature).
  - Impact - energy monitoring (MVP for electricity soon, eventually all environmental inputs/outputs).
  - Output - performance/OEE (least mature).
  - Up and running in 90 days or less, using a certified stack of sensors, gateways etc. provided by [[Konektio]].
  - "Knife and fork" approach vs. "tap and unwrap"; SaaS solution.
  - FFTs of vibration data compared against a library of previous failures to inform users of upcoming failure (which bearing, part number, when it will fail).
  - "Find it, fix it" approach linking with PTC and Vuforia AR to guide users to the problem.
  - Thingworx Analytics can be brought into AssetMinder to increase analytics capability.
  - Current focus is heavily on pumps and motors - open question over machine tools or similarly complex systems.
  - Main data input at present is vibration, with newer opportunities including turbidity, oil, pressure, and ultrasound.
  - Sensors are Bluetooth, waking every 2 hours to provide a reading; wired sensors can also output to a historian.
  - APN - Pellion secure network; question raised over how [[5G]] will impact this.
  - DMS - a maintenance service provider using AssetMinder for utilities, food and beverage, etc.
