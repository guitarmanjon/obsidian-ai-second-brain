## Attendees

- [[Steve Naylor]] (HSE)
- [[JP]] (HSE)

## Purpose

- To cover circulation of an Industry 4.0 workplace health & safety survey, use of [[5G]] for remote monitoring, project updates at HSE and AMRC, and other potential collaboration opportunities.

## Key Discussion Points

- HSE collaborating with US (NIOSHE) and Netherlands (TNO) HSE equivalents, applying to both in-work and out-of-work environments.
- Low-cost sensor solutions evaluation underway; also looking at video for activity monitoring - wearables considered? OGTC raised as a possible funding route.
- Data analytics becoming increasingly important.
- Application of wearables in medical / on-person contexts: wireless sensor network on the person; potential link with [[Dave King]]'s team in Design (medical sensors, wireless networking via LoRaWAN, low-cost sensors).
- Collaboration with University of Manchester: engagement with industry element of the work, wanting to start soon; [[Shirley Harrison]] as point of contact; SME focus considered useful, rather than just large companies; survey being developed based on literature and tech reviews, to be sent out through usual channels.
