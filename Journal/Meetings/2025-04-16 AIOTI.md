## Purpose

- [[Damir Filipovic]] gave a briefing presentation on AIOTI (Alliance for AI, IoT, and Edge - Continuum Alliance) to help AMRC decide if it's relevant.

## Key Discussion Points

- AIOTI (https://aioti.eu/) launched by the European Commission, focused on Horizon 2020 and what could be financed in that space.
- Following delivery of projects for the EC, AIOTI was set up as a not-for-profit to bring together thought leadership and collaboration in telco/cloud/IoT/Edge/AI. Supports development of EU policies. Based in Belgium.
- Facilitates building consortia for Horizon Europe (and other) bids.
- Priorities: Research and Funding (virtual worlds cPPP, consortia building, project participation); Policy and Standardisation (AI, Data, Cyber, green enablement, standardisation); Business Forum (info sharing, business matchmaking, support in projects); Events (quarterly events, specific workshops etc.).
- Membership: roughly 2/3 from Research/Academia and SMEs; some UK members including Digital Catapult and University of Essex.
- JS gave an overview of AMRC activities, including ontologies - mentioned guidelines coming soon from AIOTI, referred to as "Sarif" (spelling uncertain) - an applicable ontology.
- Next steps: Damir to send over slides; a few upcoming public events; reports to review; need to decide if AIOTI is relevant for AMRC, then whether to become a member.
