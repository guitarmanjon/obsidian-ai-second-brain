## Purpose

- Review Datta's vision, plan and strategy for building NAMRC AI/ML capability.

## Key Discussion Points

- [[Dattatraya Parle]] ("Datta") started as NAMRC's Tech Lead for AI/ML at the end of November. His strategy (shared 3rd March 2022) is built around four pillars:
  1. Infrastructure - establishing compute across local systems, HPC and cloud, with clarity on which infrastructure suits which project.
  2. Tools and technologies - enabling/running AI/ML/DL tooling, currently focused on open source with exploration of commercial toolsets via partnerships.
  3. Capabilities - building AI/ML/DL models on existing data from past projects (textual, numeric, image and mixed data, including large/complex datasets) until commercial engagements land.
  4. Skill and resource building - team building as customer interactions grow, with in-house training planned as the team expands.
  - Long-term vision: build reusable model libraries / data libraries for NAMRC AI/ML.
  - Currently working largely alone, with support from the digital team.
