## Key Discussion Points

> [!note] What was talked about?
> Capture the substance - not a transcript, but the important points, context, and nuance that you'd want to recall later.

- Connectivity:
  - C1 - Data models: two schools of thought - the UNS approach used in Factory+ vs. a meta-data/graph-structured model used by NW. NW is moving toward the graph model long-term (richer relationship modelling, powerful traversals/queries). Composites mentioned a transition plan, aligning with TMG's STAR data platform. Question raised: is it really a transition, or should both approaches be explored working together (UNS at point of data capture, graph models further down the line)? Seen as an opportunity to understand the needs of all users across AMRC and propose an approach that works for everyone.
  - C2 - End-to-end integration: progressively integrating low-level electronics, networking, and data transport with high-level data models to enable true end-to-end deployments. TMG interested in trialling high-frequency sensors and 5G. Links to [[Jon H.]]'s work on blockchain transactions in the current Catapult project.
  - C3 - Segmentable network cells (SWALE-like): aspiration from [[Steven]] and [[Grace]] to create advanced, segmentable network cells similar to SWALE - discussed for a while without real traction; question over whether it's a priority for the next Catapult year or just "good to have".
- AI / Analytics:
  - A1 - Universal meta-data model alignment: linked to C1; discussions with [[John E.]] indicate strong alignment with the meta-data/graph model, which also underpins InKA.
  - A2 - Fast, integrated AI in air-gapped environments: good interest in fast AI integration for offline/air-gapped industrial systems; NW avoiding reliance on cloud-centric platforms (e.g. Databricks) for now, though not ruled out.
  - A3 - Trustworthy AI & evidence-based verification: recent work appears complementary to what [[Lindsay]] is doing; ambition to build a stand-alone, demo-ready application/product.
- Priorities & Budget: [[Siva]]'s view is that C1 (dual-track data model/transition, combined with A1 and [[Gav]]'s digital thread domain) could attract strong joint interest across groups. Recipients asked to share their top two priorities, dependencies, and readiness (people, data, rigs).
