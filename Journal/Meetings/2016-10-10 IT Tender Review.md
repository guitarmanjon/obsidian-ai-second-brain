## Key Discussion Points

- Preferred suppliers narrowed to either ATS or Argenta.
- ATS spec'd a system over budget (~£136k) that doesn't include the cRIO element - based on what was written in the tender, they could rightly be rejected on this basis. However there's a political sensitivity: ATS were rejected on a previous tender and there's been close collaboration with them in writing this one. [[TM]] to discuss with ST and BM on how to handle this.
- Argenta proposed a system based on LabVIEW that fulfils all requirements and comes in under budget; being LabVIEW-based also means AMRC can build on it in future.
- [[JS]] to review and score the tenders properly, hopefully revealing a clear winner between Argenta and ATS.
- TM raised whether ATS' proposal could be combined with elements of Argenta's - this goes against the tendering process and unfairness rules, as a non-winning bidder could challenge a decision that picks and chooses elements from multiple bids. Question raised over whether AMRC can go back to bidders to discuss their tender (e.g. tell ATS their tender is overpriced/doesn't meet requirements) - [[JS]] to check with procurement.
- Judging notes: Sylwia said companies can be rejected for lack of case study detail.
	- Assystem's case study (section 3.1.5) was poor - light on detail and unconvincing. They also have a case study in section 2.2 of their proposal, on a system called "Assyst-Me," which doesn't appear to suit AMRC's needs.
	- Minchin and Dowsing - no case study given - reject.
	- Minchin and Dowsing / E3 - awaiting a document from Seiki Systems; doesn't fulfil requirements based on the ITT return; E3 planning also not detailed as they don't know how long it will take - reject. (Note: "Minchin and Dowsing" appears twice in the source in slightly different contexts - possibly a duplication in the original notes.)

## Decisions

- Assystem rejected due to poor case study detail.
- Minchin and Dowsing rejected - no case study given.
- E3/Seiki Systems bid rejected - doesn't fulfil requirements and planning not detailed.
