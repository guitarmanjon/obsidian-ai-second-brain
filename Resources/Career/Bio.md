## Key Takeaways

- Dr Jon Stammers is Senior Theme Lead for Data, Connectivity and AI within the AMRC's Integrated Manufacturing Group, leading a team of 15 engineers, based in Factory 2050.
- Team capabilities: Smart Factories, Data Security, Industrial IoT, Data Science, AI, Computer Vision.
- Career: started at the AMRC in the Process Monitoring and Control team (sensor/machine-tool data capture and insight), before leading it.
- Sits on the IET's Manufacturing Technical Network committee (contributes to and organises digital-manufacturing events); co-authored a book chapter on Smart Manufacturing.
- Research interests: applying data science to identify significant manufacturing performance indicators, understanding how AI fits into day-to-day operations, and the impact of new technology introduction in manufacturing environments.
