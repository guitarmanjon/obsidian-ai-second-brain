## Key Takeaways

- **Forces of flight:** relationship between the 4 main forces and how they shift across takeoff/climb/cruise/descent/landing/taxiing (e.g. straight/level flight balances lift-weight-thrust-drag; takeoff sees thrust > drag).
- **Lift generation:** probe candidates' explanation — the common "faster airflow over the top" answer is often given; follow up by asking how a plane can then fly upside down; a better explanation involves Newton's 3rd law (wing shape redirects airflow downward, air pushes back → lift).
- **Propulsion:** basics of a jet engine ("suck, squeeze, bang, blow"); understanding of bypass ratio and its purpose.
- **Other areas (background-dependent):** airframe material properties; avionics/control systems; control surfaces (elevators, ailerons, rudder) and how they redirect airflow (Newton's 3rd law again).
