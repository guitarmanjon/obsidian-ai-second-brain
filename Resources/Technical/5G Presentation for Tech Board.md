## Key Takeaways

- Planned contents: what 5G is, differences vs. 4G (incl. Nokia demo video), opportunities for manufacturing, related tech (LPWAN, LoRaWAN).
- Key features vs. 4G: ~1000x higher data volumes, 10-100x more connected devices, 10-100x higher data rates, ~5x lower latency, up to 10-year battery life for low-power devices; three use-case classes — eMBB (streaming/VR/AR), mMTC (massive M2M/IoT), URLLC (robotic surgery, autonomous vehicles).
- UK ecosystem: 39 academic institutions across 135 5G-relevant projects (Surrey 5GIC, Bristol, King's College London leading); expect 4G/5G co-existence for the foreseeable future to derisk rollout.
- Manufacturing use cases: mobile robots, remote control of factory/supply-chain processes, autonomous vehicles, supply-chain tracking — noted that LTE-A/LPWAN already deliver ~80% of what's expected from 5G, and legacy-system upgrade reluctance is a barrier.
- Sources: security survey paper (Ferrag et al., Journal of Network and Computer Applications, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnca.2017.10.017), Nokia 5G industry infographic, UK Gov 5G strategy policy paper.
