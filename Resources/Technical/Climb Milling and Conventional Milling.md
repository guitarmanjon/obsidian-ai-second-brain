## Key Takeaways

- Own drawing plus reference drawings gathered from "Practical Machinist" and home-machine-shop.com.
- Saved comparison diagram (credited "Ned Seith", home-machine-shop.com) contrasts the two approaches:
	- **Conventional milling (up-cut):** finish results from the entry cut; chip starts thin and increases in thickness; cutter starts with minimum chip load; upward force on the workpiece; workpiece travels opposite to cutter rotation.
	- **Climb milling (down-cut):** finish results from the exit cut; chip starts thick and reduces in thickness; cutter starts with maximum chip load; downward force on the workpiece; superior finishes; workpiece travels in the same direction as cutter rotation.
