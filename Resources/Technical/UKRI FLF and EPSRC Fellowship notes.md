## Key Takeaways

**EPSRC New Investigator Award (2018)** - loosely around machine learning: ML analysis visualised for easy interpretation, with user feedback retraining the model; machine tool data; feed rate scheduling (deterministic vs stochastic); chatter control; precursor to cloud-based analytics (ref. Amido); pointed towards the later UKRI FLF idea of NLP for capturing tacit knowledge from experienced engineers. Also floated: machine tool data analytics/visualisation from H5 (what's the analysis goal, can AI/ML do the discovery, who's the audience, how are visualisations produced), expanding into shop-floor data sources (micro/meso/macro), and reviewing "big data" streams (ref. He and Wang 2018, statistical process monitoring).

**H5 - EPSRC? (2019)** - large programme of work to fully understand H5 capability, with a big focus on data analytics/ML; Sandvik Edge device (Tom McLeay) flagged as relevant.

**Digi Champion 2019/20** - 5G investigation vs. existing 4G demo; more support for H5 work; FoF/KTC connectivity via Kepware and ThingWorx to quickly connect machine tools and build usage dashboards for visitors/staff (estimated 4-6 weeks).

**ABG 2019, Project 1 - H5 machine tool** - use H5 as a production machine making widgets for 2 weeks, introducing material/machine "faults" to build a big dataset for real-time monitoring/analytics; potential IMG collaboration; Siemens Edge; possible joint project with Structures (ref. email from Dave Palazon, 11 June 2018).

**UKRI FLF - initial notes and ideas (May 2019)** - core proposal concept: manufacturing data sets are incomplete, and this work would complete them using NLP/vocal interaction to capture operator/engineer tacit knowledge before it's lost to retirement (age-gap skills loss). Ideas included: an NLP system "listening" in meetings/around machines to extract key decisions (with ethical filtering - only capturing relevant talk, with an on/off control); inexperienced-operator guidance via querying an "experienced" system; visualisation capturing how experienced operators interpret data, as a guide for future users; building a dedicated data science/ML team and strategy (work packages: data mapping, data capturing, completing the dataset); adopting FAIR data principles; industrial links considered - XpertRule (Akeel Attar), Smartia, SolidState AI (Canada), MTT (Pete W, keen on data/NLP). Planned tasks: literature/tech review evidencing skills loss, publish a review paper in Y1, establish industrial case-study links in Y1, bring in an RA in Y2/Y3, conference attendance, and check alignment with any published AMRC AI strategy. Skills development: leadership and technical (e.g. a Master's-level data science/ML course, potentially funded through the fellowship).
- Prior research referenced: Rychtyckyj (Ford) on NLP tools/techniques for manufacturing; TeepTrak tablet for operator data input; Rob Gauzauskas (TUoS Comp Sci) on spoken dialogue for manufacturing processes.

**FLF administrative guidance (V5, March 2019)** - Person specification: demonstrate broad knowledge and a compelling vision; justify wider field influence; original ideas not overlapping with collaborators/supervisors; suitable host environment; approach to maximising impact (potentially co-produced with industry/public sector); ability to lead a team; secured institutional/business backing; ability to build cross-disciplinary collaborations; track record of standout research/innovation output; personal development plan (mentoring, training, new skills); strong communication skills. Outline proposal: mandatory Je-S fields (project details, Fellow, Co-Is, partners, objectives, RC/IUK relevance, 4000-character summary); cover letter required above £1.2m FEC or £138k equipment costs. Full application: 11pt Arial, strict page/margin limits, non-compliant applications rejected. University of Sheffield internal process (info from Brian Littlejohn, Jan 2019 info event): FLF provides up to 7 years' funding for 550+ early-career researchers/innovators UK-wide; open to academic/business/interdisciplinary applicants, including those returning from career breaks or working part-time; structured as 4+3 years with tapering university salary support from year 3; six calls total (2018-2020); internal EoI + 2-page CV + publications list required, submitted to Diane Maltby by internal deadline, with University sign-off needed before external submission.
- Literature referenced: Udoka (1991) on automated data capture technologies; Kassner et al. (2017), "The Social Factory" on context-aware exception escalation.

**Things to include in application / Risks (2020)** - reminders: note CIRP/AIEDAM paper-reviewing experience; costing for a laptop/PC (possibly high-GPU spec); scope of "human comms" (spoken/written). Risks: getting people to actually talk/be recorded (not a "big brother" project - consider a mic on/off control); how to access human communication data in practice.

**EPSRC Manufacturing the Future - Exploratory stream (2019)** - targeted with Prof Zi-Qiang Lang (ACSE, Hatim's ex-supervisor); funding supports a cluster of activities around one focused central challenge; SME and cross-sector involvement encouraged; sustainability and business modelling/systems analysis must be explicit; three Manufacturing Futures Retreat 2018 themes - Perpetual Transformable Products, Invisible Manufacturing (self-optimisation, flagged as most likely target), Zero-loss Systems; cross-disciplinary applicants encouraged.

**EPSRC Fellowship (Nov 2019)** - considered pursuing via the Innovation Fellowship route instead of/alongside FLF; contact was Lizzie.

**EPSRC (June 2020) Adventurous Manufacturing, round 2** - 4-page outline proposal + 2-page JoR + cover letter; deadline 13 August 2020, 16:00; up to £250k (80% FEC), PI-only. Considered pulling in ideas from a Data Science workshop (information theory - Inaki; tacit knowledge - Jeremy Oakley) plus the sonification/AR idea, floating names like "Augmented Workers using Sonification in Manufacturing (AWESOME)".

**Funding for NLP in Manufacturing (Oct 2020)** - EPSRC Manufacturing the Future standard proposal, closing 31 Dec 2020; also explored "ICT Discipline Hopping" route (contact: ict.theme@epsrc.ukri.org, and Jessica Bonham, Senior Portfolio Manager for NLP) - open questions on whether it needs a separate institution/department and whether Speech and Language Technology falls under ICT.

**Proactive manufacturing (2020)** - a broader framing encompassing predictive maintenance plus the product and its ecosystem; noted as a funding buzzword to watch.
