## Summary

> [!info] Why is this resource worth keeping?
> Notes from the TRAM (2023/24 cohort) session "Managing your own CPD", held 16/10/2023 and led by Eunice Lawton. Covers the structure and marking criteria for the ILM reflective CPD assignment, the Gibbs Reflective Cycle, and a group discussion on leadership qualities and what managers actually do day-to-day.

## Key Takeaways

> [!tip] The essential insights from this resource
> Distill the most important points. What would you want to remember if you came back to this in 6 months? Use progressive summarization, bold the critical phrases.

- Session led by **Eunice Lawton**, part of the ILM award — need to complete **6 sessions** to get the award (including this one).
- Group discussion: leadership qualities looked for in a manager — Jon's own answer: **Accessible, Open, Honest, Supportive**. Group additions: good listener, empathy, allows freedom (not micro-managing), recognises strengths/weaknesses in others/team, not overwhelming subordinates with unnecessary information.
- Group discussion: what managers do all day — answering/fielding emails and questions, delegating tasks, handling HR queries/challenges, offering support to team/colleagues, handling budgets/finances, reporting both ways (to team and to leadership), reporting to funders/grant applications, project oversight/management, representing the team.
- Source included a screenshot slide of "3 top tips" (quotes attributed to Sarge/Toy Story, Henry Cloud, and James Vincent McMorrow) — one quote was hyperlinked to "Boundaries in Marriage" by Henry Cloud, but the underlying URL wasn't captured in the transcription.
- Source included a screenshot slide on **The reflective assignment** structure (1a Personal learning, 1b Understanding others, 2 Trying things out, ~500 words each; 3a Further development, 3b Create an action plan, ~250 words each).
- **"3 examples for each section is the expectation."**
- Own annotations on the assignment: **3a** — what you've chosen and why; **3b** — 3-5 things to develop (not academic skills, must be to do with the course).
- Source included a screenshot slide on **The marking criteria**: to Pass you must achieve at least 3 points **in each section**, otherwise it is a Refer. Section 1 = reflection on learning theory with limited examples; Section 2 = reflection on opportunities for influencing the work environment through practice; Section 3 = ideas for future leadership/management development matching the CPD plan.
- Source included a screenshot slide on **Feedback**: can send one draft ahead of submission for initial feedback; feedback includes suggestions for development; can re-submit twice; a selection is Internally Verified and then Externally verified by ILM.
- "How do I reflect?" — referenced the **Gibbs Reflective Cycle (1988)** (screenshot of the diagram: Description → Feelings → Evaluation → Analysis → Conclusion → Action plan).
- **"Situation that confused me"** — reflection prompt: what happened? How could you improve next time?
- Source included a screenshot of a **Development plan** table template (columns: what you want/need to learn and why, what you'll do to achieve it, resources/support needed, success criteria, target dates) with example rows on communication skills.
