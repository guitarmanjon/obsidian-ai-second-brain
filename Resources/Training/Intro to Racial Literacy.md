## Summary

> [!info] Why is this resource worth keeping?
> Notes from an "Intro to Racial Literacy" training session dated 26/06/2025, given as a reflective exercise to prepare for a Supervisory Dynamics workshop. It captures early thinking on how key terms like equity, racism, justice, and whiteness apply to PGR (postgraduate researcher) supervision.

- Ahead of Supervisory Dynamics workshop, consider this: *By reflecting on any of the key terms explored (e.g. equity, racism, justice, whiteness etc), can you recognise the significance of the topics explored within the context of PGR supervision?*

## Key Takeaways

> [!tip] The essential insights from this resource
> Distill the most important points. What would you want to remember if you came back to this in 6 months? Use progressive summarization, bold the critical phrases.

- We, as supervisors, **may be inherently biased** in how we select potential PGR candidates.
- Introducing **justice into supervision depends on context** - who we are supervising, whether there is more than one PGR, and how we compare to other supervisors.
- **Talking about these issues collectively as supervisors** is one way to move towards a more equitable approach to supervision - not just as a one-off, but as an ongoing dialogue.
