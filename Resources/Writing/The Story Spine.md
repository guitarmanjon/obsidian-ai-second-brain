## Key Takeaways

> [!tip] The essential insights from this resource
> Distill the most important points. What would you want to remember if you came back to this in 6 months? Use progressive summarization, bold the critical phrases.

- **Beginning:** "Once upon a time…" / "Every day…" — introduces the world of the story and the main character's routine.
- **The Event:** "But, one day…" — the main character's routine is broken.
- **Middle:** "Because of that… / Because of that… / Because of that…" — consequences unfold; unclear whether the character will come out okay.
- **The Climax:** "Until finally…" — the character embarks on success or failure.
- **End:** "And, ever since then…" — the character succeeds or fails, and a new routine is established.
